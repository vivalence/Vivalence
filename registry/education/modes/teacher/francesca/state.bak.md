## Solid

- **Articles.** All seven forms, derived cold from gender + number + opening sound. Masculine bucket sort (plain consonant / s+cons·z·gn·ps / vowel) runs clean, including the s+vowel trap (sole, sabato, serpente). Feminine two-rule system automatic.
- **Plural endings.** -o→-i, -a→-e, -e→-i, accented invariable. h-insertion both directions (-ca→-che, -co→-chi, -ga→-ghe), plus the `amico → amici` irregular against `amica → amiche`.
- **Adjective agreement.** Both families, four-form and two-form, position after the noun.
- **Numbers 1–20.** Full pass clean, including the irregular seams (quindici, quattordici, diciassette, diciotto, diciannove).
- **essere / avere.** Both tables cold, both directions, families distinguished by the s-front vs h/a-front marker.
- **Negation, questions, question words.** `non` before the verb, questions by intonation, all seven of chi/cosa/dove/quando/perché/come/quanto — error-hunted and ambushed clean.
- **The three verb families, present tense.** -Are: -o -i -a -iamo -ate -ano · -Ere: -o -i -e -iamo -ete -ono · -Ire: -o -i -e -iamo -ite -ono. The vowel anchor (infinitive vowel survives into voi) is the working rule and it held cold across all three families.
- **Sentence building.** ~25 sentences produced across the families: negation, `perché` as both why and because, `a casa` fixed phrase, `in una casa` vs `a casa`, `per l'Italia` keeping its article.

## Live traps — these break under pressure

- **e-family loro `-ono`.** Repeatedly invaded by -are's `-ano` (`leggano`, `scrivano`, `parliano`). Patched, still young.
- **e/i-family voi `-ete`/`-ite`.** `leggate`/`leggano` for `leggete` failed three times in one session. The single weakest slot in the system.
- **`-ato` / `-ate` collision.** `parlato`, `comprato`, `mangiato` surfacing where voi is wanted. Past participles are not taught yet but keep leaking in.
- **`-i` vs `-a` person swap.** `mangi` (tu) ↔ `mangia` (lui/lei) trade places; same for `guardi`/`guarda`.
- **Verb *retrieval*, not verb form.** Under pressure the form machine works but the word won't come — `lavorare` and `partire` both vanished mid-sentence. Retrieval is the weak layer, not conjugation.
- **Spanish leaks.** `en` for `in`, `como` for `come`, `comer`, `hablar`, `comprar`, `habitar` (Italian drops the h: `abitano`), `mesa`, `el`, `veinti`.
- **`amici`** spelling — keeps acquiring an h.
- **Feminine plurals defaulting masculine** — `le porte`, `le finestre`, `le case` fail when an adjective or verb is also in play.
- **`cosa` / `come`.** Failed four separate times before clearing; the hook is *cosa = thing*, "what thing?". Recently clean but not old.
- **`perché`** — accent, and its double duty as *why* and *because*.
- **Indefinite `uno`** before z and s+consonant (`uno zio`, `uno studente`) — clears, then decays.
- **The noun/adjective label** behind the avere-idioms. The verb-from-label step is perfect; the label itself is a coin flip. `sonno` (noun, avere) vs `stanco` (adjective, essere) is the canonical pair.
