* Pace Layers
*What*: Stewart Brand's framework for understanding how complex systems maintain stability while adapting to change through components operating at different speeds
*Why*: Explains how civilizations, ecosystems, and organizations absorb shocks without breaking
*How*: Six hierarchical layers, each operating at its natural pace, creating resilient feedback loops

** The Six Layers (Fast → Slow)

*Memetic* - Fashion, art, trends (days/months)
- Experiments, fails fast, generates variety
- "Try this! No, try that!"
- Provides creative energy and occasional innovations

*Commerce* - Markets, businesses (quarters/years)  
- Exploits memetic innovations, absorbs market shocks
- Drives economic velocity, funds infrastructure
- Must be constrained by slower layers to avoid becoming predatory

*Infrastructure* - Transportation, education, utilities (decades)
- Requires long-term investment beyond commercial timescales
- Enables commerce, spans delayed but high-yield returns
- Physical and intellectual foundations (roads, schools, science)

*Governance* - Laws, institutions, social sector (generations)
- Provides stability and constraint for faster layers
- Includes NGOs serving "larger, slower good"
- Changes gradually to avoid revolutionary chaos

*Culture* - Language, religion, deep values (centuries)
- "Vast slow-motion dance" of whole peoples
- Invisible to youth, crucial to elders
- Most persistent and powerful constraint

*Nature* - Climate, ecosystems, physics (millennia+)
- Ultimate constraint and power source
- Can trigger "apocalyptic forces" when disrupted
- Sets fundamental limits for all human activity

** Key Dynamics

*Fast learns, slow remembers*
*Fast proposes, slow disposes* 
*Fast gets attention, slow has power*

- Innovation flows up from fast layers
- Constraint flows down from slow layers
- Each layer operates at its optimal pace
- Disruption gets absorbed rather than cascading
