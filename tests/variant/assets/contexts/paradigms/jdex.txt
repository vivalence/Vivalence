Johnny Decimal System

A structured method for organizing information by numbering “areas” (00–99) and “categories” within each area, making everything easily findable and scalable.

How to Apply Johnny Decimal

1. Define Your Areas (Major Domains)

Identify up to 10 broad domains (areas) that cover all your content (e.g., 10–19 for “Personal”, 20–29 for “Work”, reserve 00–09 for global/admin).

Create matching top‑level folders named with the range and title, e.g.:

~/JohnnyDecimal/
├── 00-09 Admin
├── 10-19 Personal
└── 20-29 Work


2. Set Up Categories within Each Area

Within each area folder, define up to 10 categories (01–10) that subdivide the domain.

Name folders using two‑digit codes and clear titles, for example: Personal (10–19)

10.01 Finances
10.02 Health
10.03 Projects
10.04 Travel


Work (20–29)

20.01 Clients
20.02 Meetings
20.03 Reports


3. Number and Name Individual Items

Inside a category, number each item/folder/file with a two‑digit suffix (01–99).

This enforces both numerical and alphabetical order.

Example (Finances):

10.01 Finances/
  ├── 10.01.01 Bank Statements.pdf
  └── 10.01.02 Tax Returns.xlsx


4. Maintain an Index (JDex)

Keep a root-level index (e.g., JDex.md) listing every area and category with codes.

Update it whenever you add, rename, or remove entries.

JDex.md example:

# JDex
- 00-09 Admin
- 10-19 Personal
  - 10.01 Finances
  - 10.02 Health
  - 10.03 Projects
- 20-29 Work
  - 20.01 Clients
  - 20.02 Meetings


5. Prefix Notes, Emails, and Documents

Prepend the JD code to titles in apps or filenames to surface context.

Email subjects: [10.01] Q1 Budget Review

Note titles: 20.02.03 Meeting Minutes – 2025-04-22

6. Review and Expand

When a category exceeds 99 items, split it into two (e.g., 10.01 → 10.01 and 10.02).

If you need more than 10 categories in an area, either add a new area range or switch to three‑digit codes.

Regularly audit your JDex to keep it aligned with actual content.

7. Digital Folder Placement

Store your entire JD structure in a central directory (e.g., ~/Documents/JohnnyDecimal).

For apps that manage their own folders, use the filenames with JD codes instead of moving the data.

Directory tree example:

~/Documents/JohnnyDecimal/
├── JDex.md
├── 10-19 Personal/
│   ├── 10.01 Finances/
│   │   ├── 10.01.01 Bank Statements.pdf
│   │   └── 10.01.02 Tax Returns.xlsx
│   └── 10.02 Health/
└── 20-29 Work/
    ├── 20.01 Clients/
    └── 20.02 Meetings/
