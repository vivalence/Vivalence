# 13.01 · License

*source-available, not open-source. free for private use; a flat, predictable license for organizations.*

Vivalence is **fair source**. The authoritative text is [`LICENSE.md`](https://github.com/vivalence/vivalence/blob/main/LICENSE.md) in the repo root; this page is the plain-language summary, not the terms.

The license aims to balance individual freedom of use and safety in contribution with the economic sustainability of the project — unrestricted private use, licensed institutional use.

- **Private use** — categorically free, forever, for a natural person acting for themselves. Modify, self-host, hack, redistribute. Self-hosting and hacking are part of the idea.
- **Institutional use** — any use by or for an organization needs a license: a flat fee per person per month at ~€12/$13/£10/¥2,000/90元, with a 10/12 discount on yearly. Licenses are issued programmatically — [get one here](https://vivalence.lemonsqueezy.com/checkout/buy/1716f8a4-2def-4373-b46a-d5447a7e3232). A short evaluation window is allowed so a team can try before buying.
- **Competing use** — building a product that substitutes for what Vivalence offers needs written permission.
- **No ML-training** on the source as training material — but using it *as* an AI harness, and using AI tools to build on it, is exactly the point and explicitly fine.
- **Derivative works** track copyright: modifications, ports, and translations of the code. Independent implementations of the *ideas* are not claimed.

Not open-source by the OSI definition, and the docs don't call it that. Governing law is Germany; Hamburg venue. This summary is not legal advice.

Organizationally, Vivalence splits in two — .org & .com. The .com is the engine, the .org is the estate. Commerce funds the commons.
