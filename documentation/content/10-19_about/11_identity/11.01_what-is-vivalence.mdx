# 11.01 · What is Vivalence

*your `~home` on any device — a modular, agentic, functional, symbolic operating system. a thin functional wrapper around language.*

Vivalence is like if WeChat, Emacs, and Hal9000 had a baby that's fair-source, deeply hackable, AI-harnessed as a first principle, and deployable in both single- and multiplayer.

- **Self-hosted, free, and Fair Source.**
- **Single-player and multiplayer.**
- **AI-harnessed and agentic.**
- **Bring your own \{any service\}.**

An AI-era operating system and application platform: procedurally generated, agentic apps — a hallucination harness for the common AI faculties and modalities. Daemons and dataspaces are composed from trait-based modes ("pluggable mini-apps") that run in persisted, isolated runtimes; a Svelte client renders them. The whole thing is a coherent architecture built from functional algebraic types — simple, powerful, very hackable.

## the architecture in three sentences

You install **Modes** into **Daemons**. Daemons are void knowledge and execution spaces, and Modes carry payloads that give your Daemons shape, behavior, face, and identity.

Modes can be, do, or look like anything that's programmatically possible.

The payloads are implemented in a Mode's traits — about a dozen so far, covering everything from APIs and harness functionality to data transport and UI rendering. The full model is [[34.01_modes-and-traits]].

The idea it's built around: people shouldn't go to applications; applications should come to people. Bring your own symbols (the Ontology, [[35.01_symbols-and-literals]]) and entities (domain); bring your own business logic, views, and services — AI providers, database solutions, authentication and user management, or any other service or API.

## where it's at

Early Technical Alpha Release. For the curious. Currently equipped for one narrow use-case — teaching a language — but expandable, as far as I can tell, to almost anything a sufficiently-specified skill can be broken into. After three years solo, the architecture is finally stable; that's why other technical eyes now help more than they hurt.

There is no security implemented yet and the system is entirely open at present — a resource constraint, with known remedies required. Run it inside a moat.

## the claim

The Mosaic-browser equivalent for the AI age — something uniquely enabled by today's technology, namely AI. `das internet dem individuum`. A saferoom for privacy and ownership against the incentives of the present internet.

- fair source, for-profit, privately held, EU inc.
- categorically free for private use, forever. self-hosting and hacking are part of the idea.
- a flat, predictable per-person license for institutional and commercial use — see [[13.01_license]].
