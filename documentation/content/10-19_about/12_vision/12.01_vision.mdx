# 12.01 · Vision, Mission, Values

*the entirety of humanity's competence — encoded, optimized, and transmitted between any two people, available to anyone. tradeoffs and all.*

## vision

Make learning anything as easy as it should be: you tell the system what you want to be able to do, and it maps your path from *is* to *ought* and teaches you every step until you've mastered it. Any measurable skill.

## mission

Be the global competence-transfer layer for humanity — the way the internet is for information. A data-driven, AI-enabled, private application system with a registry, a marketplace, and shared abstractions. Designed to become the backbone of the world's education systems, and to outlive all of us.

## the frame

When the Foundation was founded, Seldon sold it to the emperor as a colony to back up humanity's skills and knowledge. That colony needed a system to encode those skills for transit — not across space and time, but across generations. This is that system.

*In transit* means the space between our brains: the resting place where information sits after one person has mastered it and before the next picks it up to learn it. Vivalence is a system to encode and practice skills.

## values

We practice service — provide value, solve the problem.

We are curious — driven, epistemologically humble. Optimize for learning: doing is linear, learning is log. You don't know shit.

We are patient — deliberate. The system never goes out of coherence. Every work on the system is work on the whole system. One head; coherence expected.

We are dangerous — persistent, effective, efficient, sustained.

## totems

Less is More. Code is Data. Schema is God. Good is what works in Context.

Emacs for hackability and longevity. SpaceX for scale and ambition. Public education for the motivation.
