# 33.01 · The Daemon

*a daemon is a void knowledge and execution space — the equivalent of an application: self-contained, persistent, with its own types, entities, business logic, and dataspace. modes carry the payloads that give it shape, behavior, face, and identity; the kernel gives it a spine.*

## the kernel

One shape, several readers, no coordination between them. The reader picks its own fold; the writer emits flat facts.

Two directions, no reconciliation, ever. The counterpart runs at teardown rather than at boot. Order is the only precedence rule that applies.
