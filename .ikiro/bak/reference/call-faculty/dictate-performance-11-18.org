* verdict

Two causes carry the delay; every other step stays under 0.5 s.

1. polish: the repair render reasons uncapped, 5–20 s; the card and the text wait for it
2. the journal: each Activity message rebuilds every journal entry, 57–136 ms a message, ~12 messages a dictation

* cause 2 — each Activity message rebuilds the journal

A dictation sends 6 Activity messages after start (create + 5 updates) and 5–6 after end (updates + delete). Each one blocks the page 57–136 ms in =DOMWebSocket.onmessage= (=prototypes/socket.js=). A thread update costs the same (=/polish/engaged=: two messages, 67 + 76 ms). That is the lag on every click that reaches the server.

Proof, two ways:

- a no-op set of the activity store (empty → empty), journal mounted: 65 · 68 · 87 ms synchronous; 74 =childList= mutations, 38 nodes out, 38 in, all under =[data-r=journal]=
- R5, journal not mounted: 6 messages handled in 8 ms, no long frame; the same 6 cost 486–575 ms with it mounted

The store's other readers (=headline.jsx:35= · =dock.jsx:179= · =textarea.jsx:182= · =sidebar.jsx:73= · =telemetry.jsx:53=) left no mutation bar 9 on =nav.modebar=; R5 bounds them at ~1 ms a message in that layout, not timed one by one.
