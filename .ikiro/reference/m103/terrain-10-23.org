What made it work:
- *Locality*: a widget lives under the panel that uses it. A widget used by two panels moves up one level, to =bones/widgets/= or =widgets/=.
- *Context set once*: the layout called =setContext=, every widget called =getContext= itself (=+layout.svelte:28-44=), and panels passed only geometry (=rect=).
- *A panel owns a region*: each panel had a =rect= from =bridge/geometry.js:60= and its own fold state (=bridge.panels.b.$tree=).
