** 6. the OTel article (https://opentelemetry.io/blog/2026/otel-native-by-design/)

Thesis (fetched 10-12):
- a product that emits telemetry should emit it through OTel SDKs over OTLP, never a bespoke dashboard, vendor integration or polling API; the user picks the backend. Shorthand: "default to push, stay vendor-neutral, and document the contract."
- signals: logs · traces · metrics (profiles in alpha, set aside), all over one protocol.
- context must survive export: timestamps, metadata, and correlation — "log records linked to trace IDs".
- semantic conventions keep attributes interpretable anywhere; custom ones are registered (Weaver).
- two contexts: self-hosted (customer sets an endpoint by env var/flag, e.g. Keycloak, Kuma) vs platform (the platform drains on the customer's behalf, e.g. Cloudflare, Heroku).
- push beats polling (polling pushes pagination/retry/backfill onto the user).
- configuration is standard env: =OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT=, optional per-signal endpoints (=OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_LOGS_ENDPOINT=), per-signal toggles (Heroku =--signals=), sampling rate in config (Cloudflare =wrangler.toml=).
- multi-tenancy: one collector per tenant, or one shared collector routing by a tenant resource attribute.
