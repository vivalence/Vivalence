**Cascade vs native speech-to-speech for a tutor.** I recommend keeping the cascade (STT → LLM → TTS):

- **Keep Claude:** the tutor's reasoning stays on the model you already use.
- **The transcript is a learning artefact:** you can show it and correct grammar against it.
- **Control:** you pick the voice and speaking rate per language.
- **Pronunciation hooks:** a separate STT pass gives you something to score.
- **Cost:** about $0.005–0.04/min before the LLM, versus about $0.023/min for Gemini Live plus text tokens.

Native speech-to-speech only wins on raw latency and natural prosody, and it hides what the learner said behind the model.

## What changed since early 2025

- **Deepgram:** `flux-general-multi` added (April 2026); Nova-3 and Flux on promo streaming prices.
- **ElevenLabs:** v4 / v4_turbo released (2026-09-28); the Turbo family is deprecated; Scribe is at v3 / v3_live.
- **OpenAI:** gpt-realtime-2.x and gpt-live-1 are new. Deprecated: `gpt-4o-mini-tts`, `gpt-4o-transcribe`, the original `gpt-realtime`, and `gpt-5.1`.
- **Google:** 3.8 Live and TTS are new; 2.5 Flash routes to 3.5-flash-lite on 2026-12-01.
- **Anthropic:** the 5.5 generation is in place; Haiku 5.5 is a tenth of Haiku 4.5's price.
- **AssemblyAI:** its Voice Agent API is being retired.
