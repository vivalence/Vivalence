# drill-me — the locked snippets ARE the ledger

A question = two snippets; a lock picks one; the product IS the picked snippets in `ls` order (/"full of code, not full of yap"/). Locks live in the reply; a quest is a sink only when live or on his `quest` (11-06). REJECTED: rulings without hunks · a quest from memory · a lock table (11-10 /"stop telling me Locked so far"/).

## phase 1 · research — MINE, never the reply

0. **sweep** — quests · `known-issues.org` · rulings · his design · `@beef` (`maybe` = ask).
1. **terrain, measured** — the chain as it IS, `file:line`; every noun he used grepped.
2. **precedent** — one row per system holding what he is unsure of, RUN, never recalled; a handed design is read WHOLE before Q0.
3. **two approaches as CODE** — A and B, whole units, two SHAPES, never flavors of one · B = the ZERO when A leans on 'the existing route' (`bird`, 11-16); the CALLER's site first, types derived from it; each screened on `red-flags.md` + depth, a hit named in the lean; a snapshot is `JSON.stringify(x, null, 2)` of the WHOLE object, probe-printed, never trimmed · typed (11-09).
4. **the question list** — numbered, kept; Q0 = his symptom's ROOT, the shape that kills the class beats renames (REJECTED 11-14 /"think about shit coherently"/); then its LAYER, the NAME when new.

## phase 2 · the interview — one question, two snippets, per turn

```
Locked Qn: **the ruling in one line.**  + its hunks, `// path:line`

**Qm — the question in one sentence.**
<context: 1–3 plain sentences, what exists today with file:line>

Option A. <one sentence>
```js … 3–8 lines, REAL cardinality …```
Option B. <one sentence>
```js … 3–8 lines …```

Qm: A or B?
```

Laws of a question:
- **both options real, each probed** — its own key shows its expression; two to FOUR, smallest to largest, one decision each; a lock's mechanism is RUN before the lean (a schema: its suites), `unprobed` is no label (11-16 ×2). An option's ENTITY admits it by its `is:`; a trait is a tree STATE word, never a metaphor. A NEW route · helper · key greps both wire sides + the owner's tree; a reach claim reads that route's gates.
- **consistent with every lock** — `grep -rn '<its noun>' .ikiro/quests` first, a hit's SECTION read whole, never its first rule (11-17 Q29); a ruled noun, a fork a lock decides or the tree's style answers (/"match the style"/) is a LOOKUP; a rejected shape never returns; a HOME move · a lock stretched to an unnamed client · device is a QUESTION; an entity option reads its `.ts`.
- **plain sentences, compression OFF**; the two THINGS first (what · HOST · when · where today); the EXECUTOR by its owner; an act starts where clicked; a gap no caller hits · a fork he never SEES is MINE: decided, one line, never asked (11-17 /"no clue"/ · /"this seeems weird."/); else it is what he SEES (11-10); a key he never saw = its WRITER + his real value (11-16 /"what is origin?"/).
- **an option shows what he would SEE** — WHOLE: `v.object` · `.open(…)` + its signature (11-17) · fn · a behaviour = its chain, `file:line` a step, a `specimen.it` beside, never alone (11-13), ONE file, no `…`, no prose in a fence. A hunk opens with WHO calls it, WHEN, its VALUE, the sibling it differs from, its OWNER key too (11-16 /"runtime.attachment ??"/); "no caller" greps `~/.viva/registry`.
- **a snippet obeys codestyle, LEGIBLE**; a hunk's HOME = the field's writer TODAY; a choice = `.open` on the live value, env = `paladin.is`; no ✗ name in a fence, quoted or COINED.
- **a lock is HIS act, ONE SNIPPET per commit** — A/B answer · `amen` · `keep X` · `lock` commits THAT snippet only; `amen` · `go` over a LIST locks none of it (REJECTED 11-09 ×2); a KILLED name locks NOTHING — the other option stays open (11-09); `I suggest` · `show me` · a reframe = design: his words → `* open`, hunk `SKETCH`; `?a` GUESS · 'fix later' MINE · skip DEFERRED. An unlock relabels.
- **one noun per thing** — input (signal) · position (status) · record (track) never share a name; a UI word (`frame`) never names a store; a fork on today's code quotes it first; a move keeps no foreign noun
- **no carried words** — a noun I coin dies once he names it; `call it X` = EVERY layer (11-14 ×2); a noun he never said = its path, files too (11-16). `row` ✗ → `entity`.
- **his turn answered WHOLE** — every clause, a retraction's LAST winning; his unclear word is named AS READ in the lock (11-11 /"reader capabilty"/ = speech)
- **neither is an answer** — his reframe beats both; a LOOKUP before the next question; a reframed Q = the next LETTER, `Q6A · Q6B`, never a prime. A lean hiding a third option ✗ · options making his "or" exclusive ✗ (11-16); one fork twice = ONE table (11-14); two options on one UNRULED premise ask it first — as a WALK, never A/B (11-09); Q0 ends on HIS lock, a reframe re-asks it.
- **"how is X processed?"** — ONE end-to-end block, the fork a step in it; **"show me X"** — X in CODE, no lean; a change set → `show-me`.
