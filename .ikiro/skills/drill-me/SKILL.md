# drill-me — the locked snippets ARE the ledger

A question = two snippets; a lock picks one; the product IS the picked snippets in `ls` order (/"full of code, not full of yap"/). Locks live in the reply; a quest is a sink only when live or on his `quest` (11-06). REJECTED: rulings without hunks · a quest from memory · a lock table (11-10 /"stop telling me Locked so far"/).
