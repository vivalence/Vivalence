# drill-me — the goal is SNIPPETS; the locked snippets ARE the ledger

A question = two snippets; a lock picks one; the product IS the picked snippets in `ls` order (/"full of code, not full of yap"/). Locks live in the reply; a quest is a sink only when live or on his `quest` (11-06 /"no quest"/). REJECTED: rulings without hunks · a quest from memory · a quest to drill.

## phase 1 · research — MINE, never the reply

0. **sweep** — quests · rulings · his design · `@beef` (`maybe` = a question); big = read-only agents.
1. **terrain, measured** — the chain as it IS, `file:line`; every noun he used grepped.
2. **precedent** — one row per system holding what he is unsure of, RUN, never recalled. A design he handed is read WHOLE here, before Q0 (REJECTED 10-27).
3. **two approaches as CODE** — A and B: an entity or schematic + call sites.
4. **the question list** — numbered, kept; the reply asks Q0 alone — its LAYER (literal · daemon · mode · paladin), then the NAME when new (/"question zero is what we call this"/).

## phase 2 · the interview — one question, two snippets, per turn

```
Locked Qn: **the ruling in one line.**  + its hunks, `// path:line`, IN THE REPLY THIS turn (`* as code` too when a quest is live), never a landing note

**Qm — the question in one sentence.**
<context: 1–3 plain sentences, what exists today with file:line>

Option A. <one sentence what it means>
```js … 3–8 lines, REAL cardinality, helpers defined in the fence …```
Option B. <one sentence>
```js … 3–8 lines …```

Lean **X**: <one reason>.   ← /"start to include your lean in the question"/
A or B?
```

Laws of a question:
- **both options real, each probed** — its own key shows its expression (REJECTED 10-27 `"same":true`); two to FOUR, smallest to largest, one decision each (10-28)
- **consistent with every lock** — `grep -rn '<its noun>' .ikiro/quests` + each `* the dialogue` first; a ruled noun, a fork a lock decides (REJECTED 10-27 /"why would it?"/) or the tree's style answers (/"too obvious. match the style"/) is a LOOKUP; a HOME or dir move is a QUESTION; a fork names its lock; an entity option reads its `.ts` relations (REJECTED 10-24 /"clients arent per user"/).
- **plain sentences, compression OFF**; the two THINGS and code words first (what · HOST · when · where today), options after; the EXECUTOR by its owner (a press hits the TERMINAL's keymap); an act starts where clicked (REJECTED 10-27 /"orientation wrong"/); a gap no caller hits says so in sentence ONE; a report's fork is a drill question, never AskUserQuestion (REJECTED m102).
- **an option shows what he would SEE** — a WHOLE unit (a command + its `nature`), REAL cardinality (REJECTED 10-24 /"without any of the context"/). A hunk opens with WHO calls it, WHEN; "no caller" greps `~/.viva/registry` too.
- **a snippet obeys codestyle, LEGIBLE**; a hunk's HOME = the module writing that field TODAY, grepped; a choice = `.open` on the live value, env = `paladin.is`; a fence quoting tree code drops its dead words (word-guard ×4).
- **a lock is HIS act, ONE SNIPPET per commit** — A/B answer · `amen` · `keep X` · `lock` commits THAT snippet only; `I suggest` · `show me` · `design this` · a reframe = design: his words → `* open`, hunk `SKETCH`; `?a` GUESS · 'fix later' MINE · skip DEFERRED, never re-asked. An unlock relabels, never reverts.
- **a subject's RUN is its factory's** (mint · dispatch · hand back RUNNING); a consumer gets it as CONTEXT, never re-runs or wraps its vector (REJECTED 10-25 ×3).
- **one noun per thing** — input (signal) · position (status) · record (track) never share a name; a UI word (`frame`) never names a store; a fork on what code does today quotes that code first (REJECTED 10-23 /"Signal isn't state"/ · 10-24 Q6G); a move keeps no other subject's noun (REJECTED 10-26 `/shell`)
- **no carried words** — a noun I coin (`berth` · 10-25 `place`) dies once he names it; a noun the tree lacks is its path (/"call things by their name"/). `row` ✗ → `terminal` (REJECTED /"wtf is row"/ · `lines`); abbreviations written out (`$tele`).
- **his turn answered WHOLE** — every clause, a retraction's LAST winning
- **neither is an answer** — his reframe beats both; a LOOKUP before the next question; a reframed Q = the next LETTER, `Q6A · Q6B`, never a prime. A lean hiding a third option ✗; two options on one UNRULED premise ask it first.
- **Q0 ends only on HIS lock** — a reframe re-asks Q0 with his word, never Q1 (REJECTED /"q0 not answered yet"/).
- **"how is X processed?"** — ONE end-to-end block, the fork a step in it; **"show me X"** — X as HE framed it, in CODE, one tree per system, no lean; a change set → `show-me`.

## phase 3 · when he is confused — /"defuddle me"/ · /"I'm confused"/ · /"I don't understand"/ · an answered question, repeated

1. **two nouns in one hand** — a table: concept × (is · owned by · carries · changes); the conflation IS the knot.
2. **N laws, numbered, one line each** — /"relation = provenance · mime = kind"/.
3. **redraw bigger** — every entity hunk, the flows as one block; code.

## the ledger — against compaction

Every lock, that turn: row + hunk; a `Locked Q0…Qn` table re-printed each reply IS the ledger; a live quest mirrors it.

## phase 4 · the finish

Interview complete → the locked set, one fence per hunk, `ls` order; a quest only on his `quest`. The drill ENDS: no `go`.
