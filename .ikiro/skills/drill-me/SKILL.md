# drill-me — the goal is SNIPPETS; the locked snippets ARE the ledger

A question = two snippets; a lock picks one; the product IS the picked snippets in `ls` order (/"full of code, not full of yap"/). Locks live in the reply; a quest is a sink only when live or on his `quest` (11-06 /"no quest"/). REJECTED: rulings without hunks · a quest from memory.

## phase 1 · research — MINE, never the reply

0. **sweep** — quests · rulings · his design · `@beef` (`maybe` = a question).
1. **terrain, measured** — the chain as it IS, `file:line`; every noun he used grepped.
2. **precedent** — one row per system holding what he is unsure of, RUN, never recalled; a handed design is read WHOLE before Q0.
3. **two approaches as CODE** — A and B, whole units; a snapshot is `JSON.stringify(x, null, 2)` of the WHOLE object, probe-printed, never processed · trimmed · typed (REJECTED 11-09 ×2 compact leaves · dotted keys).
4. **the question list** — numbered, kept; the reply asks Q0 alone — its LAYER (literal · daemon · mode · paladin), then the NAME when new.

## phase 2 · the interview — one question, two snippets, per turn

```
Locked Qn: **the ruling in one line.**  + its hunks, `// path:line`

**Qm — the question in one sentence.**
<context: 1–3 plain sentences, what exists today with file:line>

Option A. <one sentence what it means>
```js … 3–8 lines, REAL cardinality, helpers defined in the fence …```
Option B. <one sentence>
```js … 3–8 lines …```

Lean **X**: <the deciding reason, over EVERY law> (REJECTED 11-10 less-logic on each lean /"its not our own only law"/).
A or B?
```

Laws of a question:
- **both options real, each probed** — its own key shows its expression; two to FOUR, smallest to largest, one decision each. An option's ENTITY admits it by its `is:` (REJECTED 11-09 a Literal for a painted picture); a trait is a tree STATE word (LABELED · MISSING), never a metaphor (REJECTED 11-09 `DEVELOPED`).
- **consistent with every lock** — `grep -rn '<its noun>' .ikiro/quests` first; a ruled noun, a fork a lock decides or the tree's style answers (/"too obvious. match the style"/) is a LOOKUP; a HOME move is a QUESTION; an entity option reads its `.ts`.
- **plain sentences, compression OFF**; the two THINGS first (what · HOST · when · where today); the EXECUTOR by its owner; an act starts where clicked; a gap no caller hits says so in sentence ONE; sentence one is what he SEES (REJECTED 11-10 Q8 opened on filters · kinds, not the empty deck: /"i dont get the question. unclear"/); a report's fork is a drill question.
- **an option shows what he would SEE** — a WHOLE unit: the full `v.object` · `.open(…)` · function, ONE file, no `…`, no prose row inside a fence (REJECTED 10-24 · 11-09 /"one off incoherent disintegrated yap"/ — failed twice → hook owed). A hunk opens with WHO calls it, WHEN; "no caller" greps `~/.viva/registry` too.
- **a snippet obeys codestyle, LEGIBLE**; a hunk's HOME = the field's writer TODAY; a choice = `.open` on the live value, env = `paladin.is`; a fence quoting tree code drops its dead words (word-guard ×5).
- **a lock is HIS act, ONE SNIPPET per commit** — A/B answer · `amen` · `keep X` · `lock` commits THAT snippet only; `amen` · `go` over a LIST locks none of it (REJECTED 11-09 ×2); a KILLED name locks NOTHING — the other option stays open (11-09); `I suggest` · `show me` · a reframe = design: his words → `* open`, hunk `SKETCH`; `?a` GUESS · 'fix later' MINE · skip DEFERRED. An unlock relabels.
- **a subject's RUN is its factory's** (mint · dispatch · hand back RUNNING); a consumer gets it as CONTEXT, never re-runs or wraps its vector.
- **one noun per thing** — input (signal) · position (status) · record (track) never share a name; a UI word (`frame`) never names a store; a fork on what code does today quotes that code first; a move keeps no other subject's noun
- **no carried words** — a noun I coin dies once he names it; a noun the tree lacks is its path. `row` ✗ → `entity`.
- **his turn answered WHOLE** — every clause, a retraction's LAST winning; his unclear word is named AS READ in the lock (REJECTED 11-11 Q15 /"reader capabilty"/ read as the primer — meant speech)
- **neither is an answer** — his reframe beats both; a LOOKUP before the next question; a reframed Q = the next LETTER, `Q6A · Q6B`, never a prime. A lean hiding a third option ✗; two options on one UNRULED premise ask it first — as a WALK (whole snapshots + the code), never A/B (11-09).
- **Q0 ends only on HIS lock** — a reframe re-asks Q0 with his word, never Q1.
- **"how is X processed?"** — ONE end-to-end block, the fork a step in it; **"show me X"** — X in CODE, no lean; a change set → `show-me`.

## phase 3 · when he is confused — /"defuddle me"/ · /"I'm confused"/ · /"I don't understand"/ · an answered question, repeated

1. **two nouns in one hand** — a table: concept × (is · owned by · carries · changes); the conflation IS the knot.
2. **N laws, numbered, one line each** — /"relation = provenance · mime = kind"/.
3. **redraw bigger** — every entity hunk, the flows as one block; code.

## the ledger — against compaction

Each lock: its hunk in the reply + a live quest's `* as code` = the ledger. REJECTED 11-10 a lock table: /"stop telling me Locked so far"/.
