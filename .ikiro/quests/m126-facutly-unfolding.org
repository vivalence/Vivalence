* the shape

Two threads. =actorThread= is the thread the client opened the call on: it holds what was SAID, the learner's turns (=user=) and the actor's (=assistant=). =directorThread= is its child: an ordinary harnessed thread that holds the director's work. Nothing has a third role.

The conversation is its own vector, =/conversation=. It imports nothing from =harness.js=. It calls the harness the way a tool does: =ctx.mode.harness.dialogue.stream({ thread: ctx.directorThread.id, parts })=. The harness never learns =actorThread= exists.

- The ear. It turns audio into the learner's turns on =actorThread=. A turn enters at its first word; a wordless turn never enters.
- The actor. Each sealed learner turn starts one reply: the mode's primer, the director's last word on each key, and the words of the last few turns of =actorThread=. No tools, no thinking. The learner's next word cuts it.
- The director. Each sealed learner turn wakes it; turns heard during a round wake one more round, not one each. Before a round the conversation carries onto =directorThread= what was said on =actorThread= since the last round, real role, real parts; then it hands the harness the learner's turn. The director's keys are a fold of =directorThread='s turns.

    packets.close();

    packets.enqueue(packet);
  };

    const settled = (async () => {
      for await (const packet of voiced(reply(ctx, replying, told(), steered()))) emit(packet);
    })().catch(failed);
    return (reason) => (replying.abort.abort(reason), settled);

  const directing = (async () => {
    for await (const woken of learned.drain()) {
      learned.flush();

      await carry(ctx, turns.slice(carried, learner));
