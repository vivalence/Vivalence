- the siblings :: later. /"i think they do. maybe ref too? hmm.. this would be quite extensive tho. later."/ Parked in =* deferred=.
- the registry address :: =identifier=. /"identifier is way better."/ Refused: =identity= /"thats take"/ · =address= (78 hits in the registry packages, 56 in the repo) · =specifier= (a new word where paladin has one).
