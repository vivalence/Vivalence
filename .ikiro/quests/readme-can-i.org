*** 2 — the planned half stops saying "Yes." · RULED

MEASURED off his raw block (18 questions · 9 shipped · 9 tagged
=*(planned)*=): **9/9 shipped answers open with "Yes.", and 2/9 planned ones do**
— the vault and the phone-links. **You cannot answer "Yes" to something that does
not exist.** Next to a Development Notice that reads /"Don't expect things to be
working"/, those two are the only sentences in the README that lie.

Two is small, and that is exactly why the fix is structural rather than a
two-word deletion: the =*(planned)*= tag is doing the disclaiming nine times
over, inline, in italics, at the end of a bolded question — the weakest position
on the line. Move it to a heading and it is said once, before the reader reaches
a single answer, and the two "Yes."es have nowhere left to hide.

So: one =### …eventually= sub-heading, the Q&A form kept (rhythm is the
section's spine), the "Yes." and the repeated =*(planned)*= tag both dropped —
the heading carries what the tag was carrying, once instead of ten times.

#+BEGIN_SRC markdown
## Can I…

**…run my own agent swarm in the cloud?**
Yes. Daemons are persistent, harnessed and tooled. Run as many as your hardware holds, on your cloud or on none.

**…learn a language on a system that's actually mine?**
Yes. `viva registry/tap https://github.com/vivalence/registry-education` pulls the language-learning package — then shape the surface into drills or dialogue. Swap the corpus for Latin and keep everything else.

**…use AI without depending on it?**
Yes. Modes can call on AI faculties but never require them. Without a key or a model the daemon still runs — `viva instance/doctor` lists those faculties as `dormant` and boots anyway.

**…switch AI providers tomorrow?**
Yes. Hallucinators are adapters, and an adapter is just a mode. Anthropic and OpenRouter ship today, Deepgram and ElevenLabs cover voice, and a local model is the same shape.

**…run fully offline?**
Yes. It's air-gapped by design. The lighthouse you sign in against, the database and the services are all part of your instance.

**…see exactly what runs, and what the agents do?**
Yes. It's JavaScript and Svelte on your machine. Inspect it, audit it, fork it.

**…build an app by describing it?**
Yes. A surface costs a prompt: describe it to a coding agent and it builds you a mode.

**…take my data with me?**
Yes. Shared schemas and your own storage mean no silo and no lock-in.

**…use it at work?**
Yes. Free for private use, flat license for institutional and commercial use. Fair source — [pricing](#pricing-b2b).

### …eventually

Designed, not shipped. None of the below runs today.

**…run my office on my own services?**
Mail, calendar, documents and a double-entry ledger. Each problemspace gets one shared domain, and the workflows are shaped to your office.

**…keep a password vault that never leaves my machine?**
It ships when it's audited, not before.

**…save links from my phone and read them later?**
Share to link into your own instance. It's your reading list on your server.

**…get told when a page changes, and why?**
A watcher checks on a schedule and diffs the page, then an agent tells you what actually changed.

**…turn voice memos into notes and action items?**
Record, transcribe, extract. Transcription and voice are services, so you can swap providers or run them locally.

**…track my training?**
Workouts, goals and progress, on the readiness domain below.

**…know whether my team is qualified and ready?**
Certifications, expiry dates, training records and equipment. Readiness spans people and assets, so LMS, ERP and your own training share one domain.

**…play chess against Claude, GPT or Stockfish?**
Every seat is a slot, and any model or engine can fill it. Play them yourself or let them play each other.

**…sync my apps across devices, or play with friends?**
Multiplayer modes sync their buffers across runtimes. A buffer is a game state.
#+END_SRC
