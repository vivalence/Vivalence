* Linguistic notes

BR Portuguese 1sg=3sg homonymy in these tenses:

- imperfect indicative: regular -ar `falava/falava`, regular -er/-ir `entendia/entendia`, irregular `era/era` (ser), `ia/ia` (ir), `tinha/tinha` (ter), `podia/podia` (poder), `queria/queria` (querer), `sabia/sabia` (saber), `fazia/fazia` (fazer), `estava/estava` (estar)
- conditional: regular `falaria/falaria`, irregular `seria/seria`, `iria/iria`, `teria/teria`, `poderia/poderia`, `quereria/quereria` (or `queria` in colloquial), `saberia/saberia`, `faria/faria`, `estaria/estaria`
- present subjunctive: regular -ar `fale/fale`, -er/-ir `entenda/entenda` `abra/abra`. Irregular distinguish: `seja/seja` (ser — same), `vá/vá` (ir — same), `tenha/tenha` (ter — same), `possa/possa` (poder — same), `queira/queira` (querer — same), `saiba/saiba` (saber — same), `faça/faça` (fazer — same), `esteja/esteja` (estar — same), `dê/dê` (dar — same; existing). Pres.subj is uniformly homonymous in BR for both regulars and the survival irregular set, so the "12 of 14 collapsed" pattern fits.
