First the routines and skills get better. Then they prune everything else.

* rulings

1. How a rule is written. His amen.
   - One plain sentence he can read cold.
   - No dates, no case history, no quotes, no shorthand, no pointers to other files.
   - A rule about my own tooling that he never sees leaves the documents. A guard enforces it, or it dies.
   - Code where code says it better.
2. A reply holds his subject and nothing else. No status block, no insight box.
3. What hurts most, in his order: yap over code · invented nouns · a question he cannot parse · wide code · an act he did not order.
