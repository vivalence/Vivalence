- **one unit per noun.** A noun is a directory =systems/ghost/typology/<noun>/= holding =<noun>.js= (the class; for a row also its =repository(dataspace)= factory and its =hydration= Vector), =execution.js= (the boot under a controller, beats inline, one Vector), =traits/<name>.js= one file per trait it OFFERS (uppercase export) with =traits/index.js= the barrel that IS the trait catalog (=traits.stagger(x, ctx, catalog)= reads =catalog[NAME]=, =belt/traits.js:9=; his /"still i watn a subfolder traits/ for the traits"/), =index.js= (the noun's barrel, re-exporting the catalog).
