* the law

1. Percent is the one scale: 0 = 1 minute · 10 = 1 hour · 50 = 30 days · 100 = 365 days, log-interpolated between anchors.
2. A status begins at its own percent. =PROFILE= is the preset AND the threshold; no second table.
3. =scale= is the name at every layer: driver function, note method, literal method, route, tool.
4. Direction rides the signature: =advance= (default) only raises, =drop= only lowers, =set= does both.
5. One set per call; the repository's =symbols= grammar carries unions.
6. A scale writes the retention note only: no trace, no invented history.
7. The answer tells moved from kept.
