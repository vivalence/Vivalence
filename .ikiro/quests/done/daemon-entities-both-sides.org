* the law

1. the daemon's entity set is STATIC — `literal · symbol · note · mode`, a literal on both sides (Q6 · Q10).
2. a host carries `manifest` (special) and `metadata.<route>` for every other `/metadata/*` it pulled.
3. an entity owns its row and its cast traits; a reader climbs its relations (`literal.daemon`), never a hung `runtime`.
4. a kind the client names but the daemon lacks answers 404 at first call — loud, never an undefined at a reader.
