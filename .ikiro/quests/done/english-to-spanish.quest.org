* Castilian adaptations vs the BR criteria

The BR =corpus-quality-criteria.md= hardcodes a 4-cell paradigm ("tu/vós not used in BR").
Castilian diverges:

- *6-cell paradigm*. Slots: firstSingular (yo), secondSingular (tú), thirdSingular
  (él/ella/usted), firstPlural (nosotros), secondPlural (vosotros), thirdPlural
  (ellos/ellas/ustedes).
- *Verb =known= 2nd-person convention* (new, ES-specific): tú → "you speak"; vosotros →
  "you all speak" — disambiguates singular vs plural "you" for the English learner.
- *=proficiency.castilianism=* structural symbol replaces BR's =proficiency.brazilianism=.
  Tags vosotros forms, =vale=, and (later) coger/ordenador/coche.
- *RANKED* via =wordfreq(form, 'es')=, never per-lemma (criteria rule holds verbatim).
- *Audio suffix encoding* (ñ, ü) deferred — audio skipped this milestone.

These ES conventions live HERE, not in the BR criteria doc (which stays BR-specific).
