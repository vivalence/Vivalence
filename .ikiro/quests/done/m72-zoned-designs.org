* the tree — the guiding principle

The token space. A theme holds five groups as defaults. A zone is a territory of the interface: its ground, with depth inside it (=surface= · =surface-sunk= · =surface-lift=), plus, in parentheses, overrides of any group, per key. Every =[data-zone="N"]= block emits the merged set.

* the words

A key is one role plus modifiers. Eight axes carry every word in the tree.

| axis | words | says |
|---|---|---|
| role | surface · fill · ink · on · tint · boundary · shadow | what a colour is for |
| depth | sunk · lift | where a block sits against its ground |
| state | hover · pressed · focus · disabled | what is happening to it |
| emphasis | strong · light · muted | how loud it is; the plain step is the bare role |
| entity | key · field · card · pill · disc · row · icon · label | which thing |
| signal | primary · positive · caution · negative | which meaning |
| step | 2xs … 3xl | which size |
| zone | 0 … 3 | which territory |

| role | means |
|---|---|
| =surface= | ground of a territory |
| =fill= | solid colour of a thing on the ground |
| =ink= | glyph colour on the ground |
| =on= | glyph colour on a fill |
| =tint= | a fill washed to a background |
| =boundary= | a line around or between |
| =shadow= | the tone of darkness cast or laid over |

| key | made of |
|---|---|
| =surface-sunk= | role surface + depth sunk |
| =boundary-strong= | role boundary + emphasis strong |
| =control.contrast-pressed= | role fill + state pressed |
| =control.on-pressed= | role on + state pressed |
| =signal.negative.tint= | role tint + signal negative |
| =text.ink= · =text.strong= | role ink, plain · role ink + emphasis strong |
| =shape.radius.key= | entity key, naming a step |
| =size.space.sm= | step sm |

=ink= sits on the ground, =on= sits on a fill: =text.ink= and =signal.*.ink= are read on a surface, =control.on= · =signal.*.on= · =inverse-on= on the fill they belong to. An entity repeats across groups on purpose: =key= and =field= appear in =shape.radius=, in =size= and in =control=.

* the pipe

#+BEGIN_EXAMPLE
definition                   what a theme declares — any subset of the tree, in any of its forms
  theme(definition)
    fill                     a key the definition omits takes its schematic default
    derive                   a colour the definition omits takes its rule (tint ← fill at .18)
    merge                    each zone = its ground + the five groups + its own overrides
  Theme                      static: 85 root keys, four zones of 97
    emit(name, Theme)        :root[data-theme] = zone 1 whole · four [data-zone] blocks
#+END_EXAMPLE

The definition is flexible, the Theme is static. A gradient never leaves the theme that owns it: no colour name is emitted, none is shared.

* rulings

1. four zones, numbered 0–3, each a territory: 0 chrome · 1 body · 2 island · 3 bench. The number is emitted; the name is documentation.
2. depth lives inside a zone: =surface= · =surface-sunk= · =surface-lift=. Zones nest in any order.
3. five default groups on the theme — shape · size · text · control · signal — each overridable per zone, per key; a zone owns 12 ground keys of its own.
4. =surface= is the ground, =contrast= the fill of a thing on it (a key, a chip, a row block); =contrast= never carries text.
5. an inverted block declares =inverse= / =inverse-on=.
6. selection and hover wash are =control.selected=; =focus= lives in control; radius is a scale of steps, and an entity names a step.
7. relief is a theme-root mode, =shape.relief: sunk | lift=. One pair names depth everywhere: =surface-sunk= · =surface-lift= for grounds, =shape.sunk= · =shape.lift= for their shadows. The dim behind an overlay is the zone's =scrim=, one word.
8. a pressed thing is =control.contrast-pressed=; a floating thing is =surface-lift= carrying =shape.lift=, in the zone that opened it.
9. the token space is the contract. A colour belongs to the theme that owns it; slots are all that is emitted; tailwind maps slots.
10. a theme's gradients live in its own =gradients.js=; its definition names their steps directly. How a theme got its values is its own business.
11. theme is a function with a =v= schematic on each side; its scalars live in the theme primitive's own file.
12. the definition is flexible, the Theme is static: a definition may state any subset of zones, of keys inside a zone, of every other key.
13. every length is =unit= × a step; space and type share eight step names (=2xs= … =3xl=); a role names a space step; type is absolute rem; =ring= and =depth= are px.
14. the bones are painted in zone 0, with the rest of the chrome.
15. a place takes its zone by what it is; a theme paints around that.
16. themes: northsea · parchment · porcelain · datasette. A saved theme name outside the list resolves to the default.
17. a key is a role plus modifiers on eight axes. =ink= is a glyph on the ground, =on= a glyph on a fill; the plain text step is =text.ink=; a control carries =on= · =on-muted= · =on-pressed=.
18. a glyph on fill X is =X-on= (=signal.primary.on= · =control.on= · =inverse-on=); icons read text ink, =text.light= and =text.strong= when active; the label is one entity in shape, =label.case= · =label.track=; shape holds a shadow's geometry and the zone its tone, emitted resolved.
19. the theme root is zone 1 whole: a read outside any =data-zone= resolves as body.

* the law

#+BEGIN_EXAMPLE
A zone is a container: data-zone="N" once, on the box; everything inside reads by name and never a zone number.
The token space is the contract. A definition may be anything that completes into it; a Theme is always all of it.
A theme states shape · size · text · control · signal once. A zone restates only what differs. Every zone block emits the whole merged set.
surface is the ground; contrast is what is touched on it; text is text. The three never trade places.
A colour belongs to its theme. Consumers see slots; tailwind sees slots; a gradient is seen by the theme that owns it and by nothing else.
A zone is a territory. Depth is three steps inside it: surface, sunk, lift. There is no ladder between zones and no elevation scale.
A place takes its zone by what it is, never by how dark it should look. Zones nest in any order.
ink is a glyph on the ground; on is a glyph on a fill. A key is a role and its modifiers, never a new word.
Relief is one mode per theme.
A length is unit × a step. A hairline is px. A box rescales by its unit, never by its children.
A hex in a view is a defect; a zone number in a view is the same defect.
#+END_EXAMPLE
