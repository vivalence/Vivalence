* testing schema — kill the product space

Seven strata × six ingress channels × two Envs × persistence × chaining looks combinatorial.
It is not: the system is a composition of two maps — channels→(key,value,tag) writes (BINDING)
and writes→effective view (ALGEBRA). Precedence is a total order resolved by first-hit, so:
prove each map alone and every combination is correct BY CONSTRUCTION. The 2^7 presence matrix
collapses to 7 binding assertions + the algebra laws + ONE composed walk.
