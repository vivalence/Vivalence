* Ranked adjustments + verdict

** 1. Skills lever — rituals gain invocable SKILL.md entry-points  ·  APPLY (exemplar tonight)
The empty `.ikiro/skills/` (symlinked to `.claude/skills`) is the clearest missed lever. BUT the trap: rituals are *always-on* disciplines; naively moving them into lazy-loaded skills risks them NOT firing. Resolution — **rituals.md stays the always-on source of truth; skills are invocable step-by-step RUNNERS** for the procedural ones (blast-bracket, pre-flight, live-validation) that benefit from "walk me through it" invocation. Additive, not a migration. Exemplar landing tonight: `.ikiro/skills/blast-bracket/SKILL.md` (one, to prove the shape). Files: `.ikiro/skills/*`.

** 2. Personas → spawnable subagents  ·  QUEUE (beef fork — flagship)
Formalize connoisseur/investigator/scribe/cartographer/surgeon as `.claude/agents/*.md` (per-agent tools + model, e.g. scribe→Haiku, investigator→read-only sweep tools). Real power: the investigator persona could BE a subagent I *spawn* for a finder→refuter→critic sweep instead of role-playing it inline. TWO reasons it's your call, not mine tonight: (a) `.claude/agents/` is OUTSIDE `.ikiro/` (outside the grant); (b) it shifts personas from *aspects I adopt* to *agents I delegate to* — an identity-model change. Concrete sketch for one (investigator) sits in Forks.

** 3. Orchestration pattern-naming in rituals  ·  APPLY-LIGHT (low priority)
Map rituals to the named patterns (blast-bracket ≈ parallelization; anti-rationalization ≈ evaluator-optimizer; the QA-before-blast ≈ orchestrator-worker). Value is teachability, not behavior. A short `## pattern lineage` note in rituals, no restructure. Defer behind #1.

** 4. `.claude/rules` path-scoped identity  ·  QUEUE (beef fork)
Extend the existing codemap `.claude/rules` symlink so e.g. connoisseur guidance auto-loads only on `**/*.js`. Frees always-loaded kernel context. Structural (changes how the kernel loads) + outside `.ikiro/` → your call.

** 5. Flywheel as an invocable skill  ·  LATER
The flywheel is already fully specified in `rituals.md`. A `.ikiro/skills/flywheel/SKILL.md` runner is nice-to-have; folds into #1 once the skill shape is proven.

** 6. MCP per-persona tool whitelists  ·  QUEUE (rides #2)
