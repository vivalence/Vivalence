The design (`Chess Modes.dc.html`, 65 KB, dc-runtime React prototype, `github.md`: /"styled from dapper nordic + paper themes … Modeline, tabs, chips and dock follow kajuit crown/spine/shoulder vocabulary … modes were designed from the dossier"/) is the spec. The package today (m61, compact #167): four modes on chessground with four copies of a beige `board.css`, hex literals throughout, no nav, no library, no elo ladder, never rendered.
