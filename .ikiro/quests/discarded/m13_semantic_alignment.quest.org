  seam) AND the general plugin system (modes are general, not
  learning-loop-bound) AND the daemon-side Mode entity.
- =kernel= — a module type (=type: "kernel"=, domain kernels) with its own
  registry tier history (=registry/kernels/=, flattened in M11).

Related open naming: M15 (=cake=) — the read-result term sits underneath
all three; sequencing against M15 is a design decision for this quest.
