* the critical pass — what held, what broke (rows 12–14)

Every claim in the draft quest was re-measured against disk, not recalled.

Held: the census — 23 manifests under =registry/=, owner+type+slug unique; type/slug unique even under one owner. The owner grammar — =~/.viva/registry/education/package.viva.js= is =@education/package/education= and =systems/ghost/trajectories/registry/bootstrap.js= mints =owner = "@" + basename=, so =@commons/package/commons= is house style, not a tautology. F2 — =subsystems/paladin/lifecycle/populate.js= falls the store back to =<ledger>/registry= when =VIVA_REGISTRY_MOUNT= is unset. No import map exists (=deno.jsonc= carries no =@= entries), so "imported as" can only mean the lookup grammar.

Broke: the milestone ladder. =systems/runtime/lifecycle/populate.js= =registry()= is =vip.supply()=; after the move, today's =reconcile= classifies the dead dirs as inside =<repo>/registry= and heals by =discover(<repo>/registry)= → =fs.readdir= ENOENT → every suite and every =viva= boot dies between M1 and M2; M2-first fails a fresh box the same way from the other side. Ruling: one landing. Two blast-table misses: =systems/kajuit/vite.config.mjs= watch.include =../../registry/**= (mode svelte HMR) and =.ikiro/hooks/comment-guard.sh= =*/registry/*=. One hazard the merge creates: three owners partitioned the pensieve so a cross-package slug clash was impossible; one owner leaves =pensieve.register= → =slugMap.set(version, module)= as silent last-wins, and =registry.count.test.js= "keys a slug once" inspects the pensieve after the overwrite. Live demo on disk: =registry/commons/instances/multiplayer.bak/multiplayer.viva.js= (ignored) declares =instance/multiplayer= like =starter/=, and =find.js= skips only dirs named exactly =bak=.

Forks added: F8 fixtures placement (=data/= + =snapshots/= are runtime's, not modules), F9 duplicate identity throws, F10 widened =inside= evicts an in-checkout tap silently, F11 =.dockerignore= for =commons/playground= · =commons/fixtures= · =**/tmp= (649M under =services/nlp/server/tmp= ships today; =.dockerignore= excludes =temp=, not =tmp=).
