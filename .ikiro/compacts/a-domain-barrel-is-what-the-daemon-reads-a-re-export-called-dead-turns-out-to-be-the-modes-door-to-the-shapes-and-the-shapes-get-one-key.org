Ruling 14 (quest §rulings 10 to 13, appended): the domain follows education. Doors are the verbs, one repository call each; a tool rides its door through =daemon.call= carrying user · mode · thread; the App calls the same door over the wire. Ruling 4's tool-first is a MODE's seat — blender headless, the editor's uploads — an aperture that wraps a tool when the App needs a door. Two seats, not one rule.

The law behind =schematics=, in chess's own words at =modes/board/play/types.js:3=: a mode never imports outside its directory; the domain's shapes are reached at runtime through the daemon. So a barrel is exactly =entities · schematics · aperture · tools · manifest= — what the daemon reads plus what the modes read through it — and nothing else: no folds, no generators, no private tables. The folds and the generators stay files, imported by path by the repository, the App and the tests.
