the model could not NAME a page it had drawn, could not ITERATE it, and the screen did not follow

repository verb) beef called clumsy or wrong, and every fix that landed removes the place where
the bug could exist. The trait owns the tools; the mode steers them. The response is one unit of
work; a tool's flush cannot carry half of it out.
