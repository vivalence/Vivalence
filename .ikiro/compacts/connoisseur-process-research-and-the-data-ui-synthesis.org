* The discriminator in action — what was REJECTED, and why
The deepest design work was *saying no* on the rubric's authority:
- A *Vector-based stall* (phases as leaves, signal→invoke per tick) was designed in full,
  then REJECTED: the Vector's power is routing DEPTH; four flat phases reacting to two atoms
  don't use it — incidental complexity. (effect-over-model: judged by what it would produce,
  not its elegance.) Promoted to M6 =vector-affect= for a surface that earns it.
- The *signal-=.seat=* (a route-time ambient channel on the Signal) — elegant, the third
  context channel beside input + =.use(bind)= — but Finn killed it outright: "we are not
  going to adopt that." Recorded as road-not-taken, never quested.
- The gate that pinned both to reality: "solve this function using no speculative interfaces!
  only whats apready tested and used in prod!" → the stall stayed a closure-object on prod
  primitives (nanostores + the existing observe/settle/release).
The throughline of the rejections: the connoisseur's job is the discriminator — *essential vs
incidental* — and the verdict often subtracts.

* The compact-process critique (the connoisseur on this very routine)
Finn asked the connoisseur to judge the 7-step compact routine. Verdict: adequate hygiene,
but it MIS-MODELS the process — a checklist of rules, not a fold with an algebra; it judges
*model-compliance* (followed the rules) where the connoisseur judges *effect* (does the next
session resume?). The reframe — a compact is a *catamorphism* (=session → compact=) whose dual
is the next session's *anamorphism* (=compact → resumed-context=); the pair must round-trip.
A 5-phase fold: *SELECT* (the discriminator — lossless on Finn's voice, keep decisions + WHY +
the REJECTED, drop the re-derivable, weight by praise) · *STRUCTURE* (by content-nature, not a
fixed count) · *LINK* (wire the node into the graph) · *PERSIST* (compact = full narrative,
memory = bootstrap pointers) · *VERIFY* (the RESUMPTION TEST — the missing gate). Hygiene
(no-dates, slug, retard-scan) = constraints throughout, not steps. *This compact pair applies
that fold.*

* Process lessons that earned their keep
- *The connoisseur is a FRAMER, reached at design time.* Improving the doctrine first, then
  letting the throughline + the discriminator govern every call (including the rejections),
  is why the session converged instead of thrashing.
- *Ground-truth from the code, always.* The escort "never pulls" fact, the GUN HAM shape, the
  Baserow registry — each verified in source, not relayed. The investigator's gate held.
- *Comps/specs supersede earlier picks without ceremony; the discriminator can subtract.*
  Reject the Vector-stall, kill the seat, when the rubric says incidental.
- *The naming-churn lesson (a frustration signal — heed it).* M5 cycled FOUR names
  (face-controller → glyphs → fascia → widget-glyphs) as the CONCEPT kept sharpening; the
  playground modes/groups also churned. Finn's signals — "the conoisseur doest yap this much",
  then at the close "no more half measures" / "ran an den speck" (get to it). *Lesson:* when a
  name is flagged bad, resolve the CONCEPT first (widget vs philosophy vs language — Finn:
  "glyphs is the design philosophy", "m5 WIDGETS", "glyphs being vinca ux language") BEFORE
  binding a name and re-plumbing every cross-ref. Don't churn files per naming iteration; pin
  the ontology, then name once. (=ontology-before-verbs= applied to names, not just verbs.)
  No "retard" codeword fired this session — but the churn is the kind of thing it would catch.
