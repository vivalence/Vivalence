* Lessons

- *When the human says an interface is "in flux," carve the patch to touch ZERO of it.* Don't rewrite the shared trait — find the mode-local seam. Here the reader's OWN harness running aprende's proven =hallucination.tools.slurp(mode.tools)= line reaches the model without editing the volatile =harnessed.js= or =Hallucination= at all. The flux warning arrived mid-Write and saved a clobber of beef's =soma.transcript= work.
- *Verify the live surface before baking assumptions into a big patch.* =hallucination.entities.tool= was already deleted; harnessed.js already had the thread-bind + transcript fold; aprende already ran the slurp law. Every one of my HARNESSED assumptions was stale. Read the on-disk prototype, not the memory of it.
- *Verify a suspected bug against the mechanism before "fixing" it.* =output.path === entry.absolute= LOOKED wrong (svelte vs mjs) — but esbuild's =outfile=entry= echoes the path. Checking =svelte.js= turned a "fix" that would have broken =View.mount= into a retraction. Same reflex that caught the =trace=→=dirname= REAL bug: read the getter/ctor, don't assume.
- *A superseding refactor re-points the patches built on the old layer.* The codex owns =read.js= now; m19's fs assumptions (=read.text(path, fallback)=) had to move onto raw =state.text= + =read.text().catch(NotFound)=. The codec taxonomy matters: STRUCTURED codecs (=module_=, =svelte=) cannot round-trip opaque/compiled blobs — a content-addressed store is raw I/O, not codex.
- *Connoisseur naming laws catch real rot.* One coined register (=armory=), one misapplied register (=spoken= on a hash), both fixed by naming for the true FP role and joining the existing family. The pair-at-design-time law is what surfaced that the first draft had fused the wrong two objects.
