*The line*: optional capability catches, structural dependency throws. =datamap= (:73) and =lighthouse= (:89)
stay throwing — a daemon with no entities and no authority is not degraded, it is broken. Hallucinators and
consumed services catch, because the system already models their absence: =/hello/agent= asks the cortex first
