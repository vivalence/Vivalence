  and the vdex harness index header printed the mountpoint a second time.
- `paladin.skills.fs.resolve()` folds an absolute input under the root — a PINNED law

  fired, because nothing escaped; it descended.

* his ruling, and why it is the better shape

/"id rather use the fs skills without binding root and rather than that provide more
information about the mode currently in context + ways to get that informatino on other
mounts"/

My sketch had proposed making the bind follow the trait — MOUNTED → mountpoint, else the
source dir. That keeps two roots and picks between them. His version deletes the hidden
root entirely: the only root left is the one the prompt prints, and a second mount is
reachable by asking rather than by re-arming. The vdocs/corpo pair is the case that proves
it — ONE module, kernelled twice, two mountpoints, and under a bound root the second one is
unreachable by construction.
