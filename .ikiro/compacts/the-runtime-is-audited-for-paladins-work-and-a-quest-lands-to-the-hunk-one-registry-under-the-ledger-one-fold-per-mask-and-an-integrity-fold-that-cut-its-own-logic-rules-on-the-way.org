* the law, as the quest carries it

- *Supply is the runtime's moment; the verb is paladin's.* =registry.supply()= at runtime boot, never inside =lifecycle.mount=; what =accio= returns is finished. The runtime reads fields; it never writes =??=.
- *One registry.* =paladin.ledger.registry= is record + pensieve, held once (=this.held ??==), reset on =supply()=; the record's =list()= becomes =references()=.
- *One fold per mask.* =accio(mask)= → the citizen: the module's exports under the mask's word — =manifest= and =statics= by key, =secrets= and the seat the mask's, =module= the object, =reference= the string. =wire(mask)= → a daemon's bag with =domain= picked.
- *A schema rule is a fault, not a runtime throw.* Four throws die; =settle= says =runtime.statics.remote required= when daemons; the runtime's own =Instance= (its schematics, its name) says =runtime= required at the door.
- *Seats upstream.* =Daemon.mount · url · attach=, =Mode.mount · url · bundles= seated by =resolve.mountpoints=; an entry whose reference names no type is seated in =wire=; freight root and application entry stay the module's. =mount= is the path where a thing is found under its parent; =mountpoint= is the filesystem's.
- *Integrity is a fold.* =[applies, apply(module, span) → module]=, =probe = reduce=, =span.fault= records and the fold continues, =throw= stops that module alone. Two kinds of rule: a type against its schematic, a trait against the export it travels with (=TRAITS= in paladin's =integrity.js=). No implications, no prohibitions, no notes. =accio= throws the joined faults of the module it names; the registry never throws.
