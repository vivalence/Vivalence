He is right, and the flaw was mine: I had put the row→forms resolution
in ONE route instead of in the draw. The fix removes a duplication
rather than guarding around it:

- **carrier precedence DISSOLVED into a union.** The view's set is
  =[...literals.map(fold), ...data.knowables]= filtered by =playable=.
  A conjugation ROW rides =literals= (provenance; filtered out, it has
  no TRANSLATED) while its member forms ride =knowables=. The old

  a mixed feed — the exact failure being folded away.

  inline via =conjugate()=.

  self-feed and =continuous= refill both call it, so the ontology
  guard, the AUDIO filter and the conjugation fold have ONE
  implementation, daemon-side, shared with =/feed=. The donors' bare
