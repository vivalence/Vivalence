- **Activity = reflection + roster; the Controller is the process.** One tap projects; one route controls; the client
  never folds a machine (turn 4). The demo's =fold()= is the daemon-side estimate of that tap.
- **=$entities= is the same atom kind on both ends** → one Sheet, two adapters. m64's design decision paid for the
  demo directly (turns 11, 15).
- **A held to-one is an instance; the wire's is an id.** Every reader of a relation on the runtime side collapses
  (=idOf=); the m64 =#plain= belt already did this for matching (turn 15).
- **=allowGlobalContext= is load-bearing in 29 seed consumers.** Striking it is its own quest, not a hunk (turn 5).
- **A non-persisted RemoteRepository never drops on resync** (=remote-repository.js:71=) — typology gap, pinned in
  M6/M7 by setting =persisted= (turn 5).

  not a test (turns 12–13).
