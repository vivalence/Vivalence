* Lessons
- *A keeper-with-state is a closure, not a class.* =Trunk= smelled; dissolving it into a =shard.transmitter= combinator (closed-over =let=) was MORE canonical and halved the code. Ask "what closure holds this?" before "what class?".
- *Don't reinvent the library.* =inbox()=/=Queue=, echo/frame-correlation, AND the Proxy-codec — all already in TypeBox / the channel family. =Type.Codec= is the built-in derivative; the sweep-then-implement caught the rest.
- *The schema must validate the ENVELOPE, not the cargo.* Typing =token= as =JWTToken= made the transport enforce a domain rule → the silent hang. Transport carries opaque bytes; the leaf owns their shape.
- *Concrete derivative = concrete name.* The field decodes to a =Signal=, so it is =signal=. Don't hoist to the family name (=signature=) when one derivative is load-bearing.
- *"request is our prototype."* =request.url= is already a =Url (F extends Signature)= — pass it, let the codec's Decode own the =new F=. Stop extracting-a-string-then-rebuilding.
