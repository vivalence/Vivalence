* the two truth-tables that disagree (why =is.buffers= must exist)
- =prototypes/pool.js= SHAPES patternmap: catch-all row = *"buffer"* — anything unclassified IS a buffer (the pool aggregates buffers; that's its seam).
- =gestalten/fromm/yield.js= yieldmap: catch-all row = *"opaque"* → the =object= channel — anything unclassified IS caller-facing data (the reader faces code; that's ITS seam).
Same patternmap idiom, OPPOSITE catch-alls, each correct locally. The EMITTER envelope fold sits BETWEEN them: hand =ctx.output= to the pool and a statistics object becomes a phantom buffer; hand it to the yield reader and a real buffer becomes =object=. Positive discrimination (=is.buffers=) before either catch-all fires is the only honest seat. Generalizable: when two truth-tables with opposite defaults share a boundary, the boundary needs a declared discriminator — neither default can be trusted across it.
