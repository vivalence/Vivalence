* arc

1. *the defect and its half-revert* (rows 41–44). The subscriber is the ONE deriver of =ontology= and =symbol=,
   and it fires only on an ORM changeset with the collection loaded. The corpus rework had bought its speed
   twice — fork-and-clear per chunk (the real 132s→20s fix) AND raw pivot SQL (the regression). Only the second
   went back. =from.assign({ updatedAt: new Date() })= stays load-bearing: a collection-only change gives the
   literal no changeset. Measured: 2.0s over the live-shaped copy, 1.9s fresh. "Not manual" became a stamp
   rule: the installer's own source is an input to the install stamp, so an installer change is a reinstall.
2. *the environment fights back* (rows 44–46). Three boots refused before one =awk length($2)= showed six blank
   secrets in the instance =.env=. The ladder read a blank as a voice; the verdict read it as unset; the writer
   never wrote one. Then beef's own =instances/use ital= sat under a repo-root =.env= pin — the fold was right,
   the cwd overrode it, and =instances/list='s =*= marks the EFFECTIVE mount, not the selection.
3. *one law, at the funnel* (rows 47–48). beef wanted the fix outside typology. =split(bag)= is where every
   bag enters paladin, so the blank bucket lives there, beside the writer's law in the same subsystem. The
   typology =Env= stays a pure fold. Cost: the doctor no longer dims a blank line as a shadowed voice.
4. *the next gate down* (rows 49–53). Optional key → open gate → provider crash at =population.services= with
   the wrong words. m52's dormancy generalized by one slot: a consumed service with a blank secret is dropped
   at mount and named, exactly like a hallucinator. The proof came from beef's own watcher, which rebooted on
   the edit and healed the 6583 rows in the same breath.
