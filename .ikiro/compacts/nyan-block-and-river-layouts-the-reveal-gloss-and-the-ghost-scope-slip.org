* NYAN — the two live bugs beef caught (fixed against screenshots)

*Cursor drift + two bars.* First river cut put the caret in the STAGE (=frame=stage=) measuring the transformed track via getBoundingClientRect — the caret's own 0.07s transition raced the track's 0.18s glide → drift; and the faint =.focus= centre bar read as a second cursor. beef: *"like.. what is this??!! the cursor is drifting too! weird."* Fix: the caret now lives INSIDE the track (=frame=track=) — it subtracts the track's own rect so the translate cancels, and the glide CARRIES the caret with the words (no desync); =.focus= bar removed.

*Off-centre on load.* The first word sat off-centre because =offsetLeft= was measured before the monospace font loaded (FOUT mis-measure). Fix: both the track-centre and the caret re-settle on =document.fonts.ready= + a =ResizeObserver= on the container.

Also this pass: font =xl→lg=; depth floor =0.12→0.4= + gentler step (beef: *"dont occlude past and present so aggresively"*).
