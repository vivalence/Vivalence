* arc

** the checker's grammar has one dimension and the gloss has two

"he/she is / stays" carries a pronoun group (tight slash) and a verb group (spaced
slash). =separate()= splits on bare =/=, so both groups collapse into one flat
alternative list and the cross product is unrepresentable — a single-word alt borrows
a tail from its nearest multi-word RIGHT neighbor and a head from its nearest
multi-word LEFT neighbor, once, from raw alts only. "he is" and "she stays" fall out;
"he stays" cannot. Proven by running the real =matches()= against the real gloss
before any fix talk. The fare-gloss edits this session (rows 4–5) narrow the blast
radius — "we do / we make" passes on exact-alt match — but every two-group gloss in
every corpus still mis-rejects until =separate()= learns groups. DESIGN PENDING, beef
picks the shape.

** the data fixes are small and the law held

All five edits are English-side or audio-side; the one Italian text change
(=ho-quasi-fatto=) is the audio-verbatim law working as intended — the recording says
"finito", so the text follows the audio, not the other way around. The slug/filename
keep the stale spelling because renaming a slug orphans retention rows; surfacing that
trade instead of silently renaming was correct. Dataset edits reach no daemon until
