* the port thief (carried over the fold)

/"when i run multiple cycles of viva instance/run ctrl+c cancel then some processes dont die
and i get this error"/ — =Error: Port 1794 is already in use=.

The hypothesis died under measurement. =instance/run='s whole spawn chain shares the
terminal's foreground pgid, so ctrl+c reaches every child including esbuild — and machine-wide
there were ZERO deno/vite processes with ppid 1. The holder was a *sibling Claude session's
background task* (=claude daemon → bg-spare → zsh 85391=) running kajuit's vite under its own
process group, invisible to any terminal ctrl+c, ~5 minutes old. Every subsequent run cycle
failed kajuit on the bind and read as "didn't die". beef ran =/bin/kill -- -85391= via =!=;
verified: port free, group dead. Teardown exonerated, nothing to fix in ghost.
