* why openrouter was never there

#+begin_example
instance.viva.js      hallucinators: [anthropic, openrouter]          2 entries authored
  ↓ paladin instance.mount()
  environment()       claims ~/.viva/.env → SECRET_VIVA_OPENROUTER_API_KEY absent
  resolve()           fires the thunks   → secrets.key = undefined
  settle()            instance.js:139  filter(alive)                  openrouter DROPPED
                      dormant.push("daemon[hello].hallucinators[1]")
  ↓ runtime populate.js:21   for (const mask of paladin.instance.daemons)
  die.mask.hallucinators = [anthropic]                                1 entry
  ↓ population.js accioMap(registry)
  vip resolves ONLY @commons/hallucinator/anthropic
  → openrouter/provider/index.js never IMPORTED. body never runs. no logs.
#+end_example

The consequence he needed: a =try/catch= in =acid= cannot catch a missing key, because that entry is gone
before =acid= runs. Blank secret is =dormant=; a provider that throws is =refused=. Two ledgers, two causes —
which is why the =refused= design from the previous fold is still owed and still correct.
