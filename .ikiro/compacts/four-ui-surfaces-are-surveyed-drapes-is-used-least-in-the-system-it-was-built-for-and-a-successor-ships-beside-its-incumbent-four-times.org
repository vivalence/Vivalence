** the survey (52) — four surfaces, measured

No file written, by order. What it established, with the measurement beside each:

*** dapper ships two colour contracts

=lib/flatten.js= emits both on every =design()= call:

#+begin_example
--colors-skeleton-N-*   :root[data-theme=X]   N ∈ 0..4   ~20 consumer files
--zone-*                .zone-N               N ∈ 0..5   ZERO consumer files
#+end_example

Zones are the successor and the better model — six named roles written out at =themes/nordic.js:5-11=
(=canvas · panel · recess · raised · accent · overlay=), =ZONE_COUNT = 6= at =lib/flatten.js:1=, pinned by
=tests/zone-emit.test.js= across six zones × seven roles × three states plus an error triplet. Then =nordic.js:94= sets
=.zones= and =nordic.js:100= sets =colors.skeleton = {0..4}= under a local literally named =compat=.

The arity is the tell: zone5, =overlay=, has no compat twin. The one zone a mode reaches for — popovers, modals, the
chess end card that landed in #177 — is the one the live contract cannot name.

*** dapper carries =primitives/= as a dead, DIVERGED fork of =lib/=

=colors.js= 391 vs 452 lines, =tokens.js= 128 vs 130 — differing, not stale copies. =builders.js= identical. Live path is
=lib/=: =mod.js= re-exports it and =lifecycle/index.js=, the postcss door named in =deno.jsonc=, imports
=../lib/system.js=. =primitives/= is reached only by =belt/postcss-plugin.js= and =tests/builders.test.js=, and
=belt/index.js= is exported from neither =mod.js= nor =deno.jsonc= — so that belt is a second dead door. Two palettes on
disk, differing, nothing naming the winner.

*** drapes =context/= is an orphan spelling the contract that is itself due to retire

=decorum.js=, =Zone.svelte=, =SkeletonProvider.svelte=, =useSkeleton.js= — zero importers outside =subsystems/drapes/=.
=Zone.svelte= re-declares sixteen CSS variables by hand onto a =display: contents= wrapper, which is a naming shim over
what the cascade already does, and it spells =--colors-skeleton-{n}-*=. Adjacent: =skins/= is 7 components plus a =bak/=
of 7 more, with two consumers repo-wide taking one each — =education .../dataspace/Dashboard.svelte:3= takes =Filter=,
=anima .../panels/h/Inspector.svelte:6= takes =Skin=. It is the one namespace export the drapes readme carves out.

*** what drapes is ACTUALLY for, measured across education's 33 importing files

=Desk= ×10 · =ViewportLock= ×8 · =stage= ×4 · =Pip= / =Keyboard= / =Button= ×3 · =visible= / =preferences= / =Tag= /
=Shelve= / =Input= / =Card= / =Asset= ×2. Layout and viewport plumbing. *Nobody uses the semantic component pitch* the
readme leads with — no =<Text as="h1" variant="heading" size="3xl">= anywhere, and the whole BSP =triage= story
(=Plane= / =Shelve= / =Box=) shows 2 uses.

*** droneaid — the best-composed view of the four, and the only one that cannot be themed

One mode, =modes/assembly/pt10=, 1218 lines with =Assembly.svelte= 516 of them. Raw three.js + OrbitControls, no threlte.
Step is a single piece of state: =show()= moves camera, explode and scroll together, =retain()= persists it to the
buffer, and an =$effect= subscribes the buffer back — bidirectional without a loop, because =show= is guarded on
=!== step= and =retain= on =!== buffer.data.step=.

Against that: 13 hardcoded hex vars at the =.assembly= root and =Archivo= pulled from Google Fonts in =<svelte:head>=.
Zero dapper, zero drapes. Switch nordic → paper and droneaid stays white-studio.

The contrast that names the choice is next door: chess solved the identical problem at =kit/Theme.svelte= by aliasing
dapper tokens into short local names (=--s1=, =--sq-dark=) inside =:global(.chess)=, shipped as a COMPONENT so the
bundler cannot drop it. Same ergonomics for the view author, and it follows the theme. Droneaid's hex is defensible for
the illustrated surfaces — an assembly studio wants the IKEA look — but =--line=, =--hover=, =--mute=, =--paper= are
chrome.

Two rough edges recorded: =world.byId[id].swatch= is read unguarded in the chips loop, so a guide step naming a part the
GLB lacks throws mid-render instead of landing in =fault=; and =explode= is read back from the world on a 100 ms
=setInterval=, a value the world could push.

*** the language-learning UIs — 67 views, 18k lines, uneven

Token discipline holds where it counts: 50 of 67 files on =--colors-*=, 11 carrying hex, and the hex is concentrated in
ILLUSTRATION. =Riddler.svelte= draws a jester in CSS — =.cone.gold=, =.pom.red=, a three-stop backdrop gradient. That is
palette-as-artwork and the no-hex-in-a-view rule should not be read onto it.

Riddler is nonetheless a stub by its own admission — header comment: /"gutted to the bare minimum that RUNS … Everything
else was stripped."/ 538 lines, no drapes, no tokens, =console.log= on both sides of the aperture call, the riddle read
once at module scope from =buffer.data?.riddle= so a new riddle never reaches the view, and it is the only view in the
survey that reaches =buffer.mode.connection.call("/assistant/message")= directly instead of through =mode.call.*=.

Dojo is where the real design lives — 793 + 1042 + 536 + 516 + 384 + 368 + 287 across =Dojo.svelte= and six =parts/=,
10 drapes imports, 18 files on tokens, zero hex. The only mode with a decomposed view tree, and =Builder.svelte= at
1042 lines says the decomposition stopped one level short.

And the copied skeleton: =cloze=, =match=, =judge=, =exhibit=, =impara= are each exactly =drapes=1, tokens=1,
styleblock=1=. One skeleton, five copies, diverging. Chess met this and answered with the kit-copy law plus
=tests/kit.test.js= pinning the copies byte-equal. Education has the copies without the pin.

*** anima — cleanest colour, least component reuse, worst naming

*Zero hex across all 56 views.* 50 use =var(--)=, 51 carry a scoped =<style>=. The token law holds perfectly, in the
system the law was written for.

And drapes barely exists here: 9 of 56 files import it, taking =Section= ×4, =Icon= ×2 and singletons of =Pip= /
=Markdown= / =Json= / =Frame= / =Chip= / =skins=. Anima hand-rolls its chrome against dapper's variables and skips the
component library.

The anatomy naming is coherent where it is anatomical — =bones/= is crown, spine, shoulder, pincer — and empty where it
is not: =panels/a..h=. Only A/B/C/G/H mount in =+page.svelte=; D/E/F are tabs inside C, dispatched at =c.svelte:296= by a
raw =({#if index === 0}…{:else if index === 1}…{:else})=. Panel identity is recoverable only by opening the file, where
the chess kit next door says itself — =Shell= / =Board= / =Moves= / =Seat= / =Nav=.

=Dock.svelte= is 1845 lines with 122 =class="= sites and no =widgets/= beneath it — the largest view in the repository by
3.6×, and the mechanical form of the frontier's /"still ... ish. bit convoluted"/.

=+layout.svelte= is dense but honest: five contexts built inline, a =computed()= over two lighthouse atoms driving a
five-state =gate=, subscriptions unwound by hand in the =onMount= return. One thing to fix — the =window.__viva=
devtools hatch is marked =@beef Temporary= and ships in the prod bundle UNGUARDED, while the logger tap three lines
below it is correctly behind =import.meta.env.DEV=.

=systems/anima/README.org= is 0 bytes. The only one of the four surfaces with no written intent, and the one whose
architecture beef has flagged.

*** the verdict the survey closed on

Four surfaces, one gradient, and the two axes run OPPOSITE ways:

#+begin_example
colour discipline   anima 0 hex/56  →  education 11/67  →  droneaid all-hex     (improves inward)

Root cause written as ONE move made four times: ship the successor beside the incumbent, test the successor, migrate

surface under the same law that protects =quests/bak/= — but it shares the symptom exactly: every census of the package
double-counts unless the walker excludes it by hand, and no convention tells a walker to.

Secondary drift recorded in the same entry: =drapes/readme.org= documents =themes/dark.js= (disk has =nordic.js= /
=paper.js=), a =Modeline= component that exists nowhere but the readme, and =bsp.css= / =fonts.css= exports that are

Three rulings left for beef, none of them repairs: pick a colour contract and delete the other with =primitives/=;

** a green test proves the code runs, never that anything calls it

Four times on disk in these trees. =zone-emit.test.js= passes on CSS with no consumers; =builders.test.js= passes on the
dead =primitives/= fork. The missing check is not another assertion — it is a CONSUMER GREP, and no suite runs one.
Before believing a subsystem is live, count its importers from outside its own directory.

** a recovery surface must announce itself to walkers, or every census lies

=bak/= is protected by law and invisible to measurement. Education's is ~4.1k lines of =.svelte= plus a full mirror tree,
about a third of the package's view surface, and every count taken over =modes/= is wrong by that much until someone
remembers. The law is right; the convention that tells a walker where it applies does not exist yet.

** a mode-local palette is a THEME ALIAS, not a colour list

Chess and droneaid met the same problem — a package's views want short, local colour names — and answered it opposite
ways. Chess's =kit/Theme.svelte= aliases dapper tokens into =--s1= / =--sq-dark= inside =:global(.chess)=, shipped as a
component because the bundler drops a =.css= import. Droneaid declares 13 hex values at its root. The author's
ergonomics are identical; only one follows the theme. Alias the token, never copy the value — and if the surface is
genuinely illustration, say so at the declaration so the next reader does not "fix" it.

** measure the thing the sentence is about

The survey's two most useful facts — drapes at 33 education files vs 9 anima files, and =--zone-*= at zero consumers —
are both counts of CONSUMERS, and neither is visible from inside the package being judged. Reading =dapper/= tells you
zones exist and are tested. Only =grep -rl= outside it tells you they are unused.
