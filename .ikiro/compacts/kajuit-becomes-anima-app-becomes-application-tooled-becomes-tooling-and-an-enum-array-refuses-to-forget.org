* laws

- A rename's blast is =grep -r= over the working copy, never =git grep=, and its pattern is a
  word grep widened past the shapes the plan names. The sites the plan did not predict are the
  ones that break.
- An enum-array column migrates by its VALUES, not its schema. Removing a member breaks every row
  still holding it at HYDRATION, before any boot code runs. Keep the old member deprecated at the
  bottom until every instance has booted once (beef, 140).
- A pass closes with =test= AND =fixtures=: every snapshot suite in compare mode, the live ones
  HOT, a grep of fixtures/ + snapshots/ → zero. A green suite never proves its fixtures fresh.
