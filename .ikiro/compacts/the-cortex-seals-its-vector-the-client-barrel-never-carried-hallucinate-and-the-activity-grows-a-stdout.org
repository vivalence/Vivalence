* laws

- *A =yields= edge IS the subscription.* Never hand-roll a stream leaf: =strip= carries =yields=, =wire= projects =connection.stream=, =http= frames the generator as SSE, and the client reaches it through the same =daemon.call= road as every call leaf.
- *The entity projects, the controller owns.* =controller.stdin/stdout= are the Vector and the Span; =activity.stdin/stdout= are the ends an entity exposes. A repository owns neither.
- *A state change is a verb, not a second record.* The machine folds =open · pause · resume · stop · close · fault · abort= into the status; logging the fold as well doubles every line.
- *Tap before you snapshot.* A generator's body does not run until its first =next()=, so a backlog read that "then subscribes" drops whatever arrives in between.
- *=settled= is not the end of the stream.* It fulfils on the same tick as the closing mark; a reader that ends on it loses that mark. End on the machine's =settled= state, after yielding.
- *The client barrel is the served artifact's barrel.* A constructor that reaches for a shard runs in the browser too; =index.client.js= is 10 of 16 namespaces on purpose, and moving work into a constructor moves it across that line.
- *A copy is not a mount.* =~/.viva/instances/<slug>/= drifts from =commons/instances/<slug>/= silently, and there is no sync verb — before a demo, diff them.
