* Lessons
- *Chrome + a real token beats a week of inference.* The raw =new WebSocket= probe (server answers the handshake) collapsed the search space from "server or authorize or token or client" to "client only" in one call. curl proved the multiplayer NetworkError a red herring; the deno =Url= probe cleared the query-token suspicion. Instrument the boundary, don't theorize across it.
- *The bug was never worth fixing — the prototype was worth deleting.* Four escalating folds (nest → session → slurp → recursion) all tried to PRESERVE =Conversation=. Each passed the "does it work" bar and failed beef's nose. The question that dissolved it wasn't "how do we transport it" but "why does it exist." When the fold keeps getting rejected, question the thing being folded.
- *"is X used during Y?" is a scoping scalpel.* One Socratic question (is Socket used during multiplexing?) killed the entire recursion branch by exposing that the abstraction already existed, empty and unused.
- *Handshake/negotiation is dead weight when the protocol is static.* =Conversation='s shape-exchange negotiated what each side can receive — but the channels never vary. Negotiation earns its keep only under genuine variance; here it was ceremony.
- *Composability check: does the new thing subsume an old one?* harness→hallucination + connection-streaming + reactive entities didn't just enable the dialogue — they made =Conversation= redundant. New spine landing = audit what it obsoletes.
