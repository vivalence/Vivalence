* one breath

The V3 vdex pass went from scratch to the shelf on his go, then his own screenshots drove three rounds of
repair: an invisible menu (a design zone this system does not define), a raw editor whose three layers each
ran on a different rhythm, and a pdf that never drew because the cargo route dropped every path with a space.
Then he asked whether the same module could be kernelled twice on two mountpoints — and the answer was four
defects deep, ending in the mode↔literal owner relation he had designed months back and never landed. It
landed. The session closed on a correction I earned: I called the =vdex= symbol a description of the MODULE.
It is the ONTOLOGY marker. He had told me that three turns earlier and I wrote it wrong anyway.

** two mounts of one module (25–42)

/"do you expect this to work?"/ — the same module kernelled twice, once under the manifest slug, once under a
custom one. No, and it took three of his corrections to get the frame right:

1. /"should, no?"/ · /"aka same mode, loaded twice."/
2. /"actually, the vdex as ontology is CORRECT! ontology is the same. i just want a second mountpoint."/
3. /"no. youre still considereing ontology level. this is purely mode slug."/

He was right each time. The ontology is the KIND; a vdex document is a vdex document on any mount. What was
missing is that **nothing on a row and nothing in a query named the mode**.

Then /"literals should carry modes?! dont they?! i thought they did. i recently designed that … check quests."/
— and he had: =m53-vcompany.pre.org:34=, /"New relationship between mode and literals, mode and symbols. I
think I want that on typology level."/, with the sketch under a GATED heading and his own diagnosis in it:
today a dataset row has no owner, so prune is impossible everywhere. Also =Literal.ts:10= carried his marker,
=// @beef: owner relation to mode=. And /"wtf is home()?? runtime/typology level??"/ — no: six lines in his own
package aperture, reading a coordinate symbol's path to place a document.

The order he then gave, and what each part cost:

- **=mask.slug=** — =Mode= takes =this.slug = this.manifest.slug=, and =population.js= read =mask.statics=,
  =mask.secrets= and =mask.mountpoint= but never =mask.slug=. Both entries came up =vdex=, computed the same
  mount branch, and one overwrote the other in =modes[type][slug]=. One mode, on whichever mountpoint loaded
  last. Fixed, and the mode ROW is ensured on the effective slug.
- **the owner relation** — his sketch was m:n both ways; his ruling at the landing was /"just one … one mode
  per literal/symbol"/ · /"single owner"/. So =m:1= on literal and symbol, =1:m= on mode, schematics mirrored,
  and the unique key on both moved from =slug= to =(slug, mode)= — without that the two mounts cannot both
  hold =32.01=, because the slug is the identity every verb uses.
- **the crash, and the rule it taught** — the first cut owned everything a mode installed and broke his
  runtime: /"Literal "10.00.about.mdx" must have exactly one TOPOGRAPHICAL symbol, found 2"/. The =vdex= symbol
  is the ONTOLOGY marker. One ontology, however many mounts carry it. Scoping it per mode minted a second copy
  and a document ended up holding both. The rule: the declared scheme (the ontology marker, the formats, the
  slots) is shared and unowned; what a mode COMPUTES is that mounting's own reading of its world and is owned.
- **every read scoped** — boot (rows · coordinates · orphans · prune · report · upsert · reconcile), the index,
  the aperture including =home()=, the tools, the harness, the emitter, and the App's two subscriptions. Prune
  became the mounting's own.

Measured on his daemon after the migration: =office/vdocs= 27 literals · 50 symbols · 27 linked;
=office/corpo= 33 · 33 · 33. In the db: 27 owned by one, 33 by the other, 0 unowned; 26 scheme symbols shared,
51 coordinates owned, 0 unowned.

** a path is not a url — twice in one session

=paladin.skills.resolve= built =new URL(path, "file://…").pathname= and returned it as a FILESYSTEM path.
=URL.pathname= escapes, so =vdex_file= with a spaced title wrote =Notes%20on%20invoices.md= to disk and the
next =Deno.stat= missed the directory it had just been handed. Same disease as the cargo route, second site.
Fixed the same way; paladin 41 passed · 180 steps.

TWO instances in one session is a pattern, not a coincidence. A third site is worth a sweep before it is found
by a screenshot.

* the failures worth keeping

- **the invisible menu** — a design token that does not exist here fails SILENTLY. CSS has no undefined-variable
  error; the rule simply does not apply. Every design zone must be mapped to a token this system defines before
  the first paint, not after the screenshot.
- **three layers, three rhythms** — the raw editor desynced because a gutter, a paint layer and a textarea were
  three separate flows that only LOOKED aligned in the first few lines. Anything that must line up should share
  one flow, not three that agree at the top.
- **owning the vocabulary** — scoping by mode is right for what a mount reads and wrong for what the ontology
  declares. I applied one rule to both and crashed his runtime. The distinction was already in his design
  (/"a symbol declared by two modes is multi-owned"/) and I did not read it closely enough before writing.
- **the wording that earned the shout** — after he corrected me twice that ontology ≠ mode, I wrote that the
  =vdex= symbol "describes the MODULE". It marks the ontology. Getting a fix right and then describing it in
  the vocabulary he had just rejected is its own failure, and the loudest one here.
