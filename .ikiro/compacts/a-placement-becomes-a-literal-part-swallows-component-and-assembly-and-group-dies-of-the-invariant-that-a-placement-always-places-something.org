* the schema, as it stands

#+begin_src js
part        what a thing IS       LABELED · SOURCED · MODELED · PARAMETRIC · COUNTED · SEQUENCED
placement   one occurrence of it  LABELED · PLACED · JOINED
step        one layer of change   LABELED · INSTRUCTED · PREPARED

PLACED { layer, name, parent?, ref, translation?, rotation?, scale?, active?, params? }
#+end_src

- *=ref= is never absent.* That single invariant is what killed =group=: a placement that places nothing is not a placement. A blender collection mints a part plus a placement of it; so does an =Empty=. No kind, no tag, no hole.
- *A placement's slug is =<layer>.<path>=*, so the same layer+parent+name again IS the same row — an over by construction, not by a merge rule someone has to remember.
- *Two ways to nest, never blurred.* =parent= is structure inside ONE layer, not reusable. =ref= naming a part that has an interior grafts that composition under the placement's path — that is reuse, and it is how the propeller-inside-a-motor is described once for four motors.
- *=JOINED= rides the fastener's placement.* /This/ bolt holds /these/ occurrences at /this/ torque; a part number is never loose or tight. The joint's kind is a =joint.*= symbol on the row, not a field — one vocabulary, not two.
- *Top level is derived, never stored.* A part nothing places is a root. Nothing to mark, nothing to keep in sync.
- *=uses= / =in= became the spine.* It used to be synthesised by scraping slugs out of trait JSON. Now a placement =uses= its =ref= AND its =layer=, so =in= answers /where is this part used/ off the row, =closure= pulls a layer's interior off the reverse edge, and a cycle is refused at the WRITE — /"already contains X — placing it here would eat itself"/ — instead of at the read.
- *=bom= seals a bought sub-assembly.* Every placement of a =COUNTED= part counts, and nothing inside one that has its own interior. A bolt merely parented under an arm still counts: arrangement is not containment. This is the case that made the fold correct rather than merely tidy.

* the failures

- *I argued the wrong side first, twice, and both times from authority.* I had told beef a node is only an edge — no identity, no reuse, layering dies, count explodes. Three of those four are true of an edge and none of them survive contact with glTF, USD, three.js or any CAD assembly tree, all of which make the occurrence first-class. Then, one message before he dissolved them, I recommended /keeping/ =component= and =assembly= — the exact union-by-absence he had thrown out two days earlier and which I had helped him name. *A defect you have already ruled against does not become sound because you are the one leaning on it this time.*

My earlier edit had targeted a string that no longer matched and I never verified the replace. Nothing enforces an output shape on the direct call path, so eight suites stayed green over a lie.
