* what the repositories provide, and what the drawer now speaks

The brief was one sentence — /"adapted closely to what the repositories provide - domain
and runtime … literal and symbol on both"/ — and the census is the whole design:

- runtime =LiteralRepository=: =symbols= (array = AND · ={$all,$in,$none}=), =search= over slug + both translations.
- runtime =DataRepository= (base of literal AND symbol): =traits= string · [] · ={$contains,$overlap,$none}=.
- domain =LiteralRepository=: =feed · due · novel · byStrength · byLastSignal(signals) · sample({status})=, =card= (status from the user's retention), =constrain=.
- symbol repository (runtime = domain): =slug=, =trait.LABELED.name=, =traits= ONTOLOGICAL/STRUCTURAL/TOPOGRAPHICAL, =findByIdentifiers=.
- entity fields: =ontology= (already a TOPOGRAPHICAL symbol chip), =rank=.

So the three bins take symbol chips AND trait chips — the two grammars have the same three
buckets, and one gesture carries both (=symbols.$all · traits.$contains= / =$in · $overlap= /
=$none · $none=, printed under each bin as the stored keys, not the design's =$every=). Take
= the domain streams by their own names (=by strength=, =last signal= with signal toggles,
=sample= with status toggles). 04 window = =rank= and =search=. Symbol search is the symbol
repository's own =find= (slug LIKE, LABELED name LIKE, =traits.$overlap= kind filter),
server-side per keystroke — the 2600-row payload of yesterday is gone. Nothing was invented;
=omit= died because nothing in the design or the repositories asks for it.

* the drawer — what changed against the mock, and why

The mock is the commission (m24's rule); the deviations are content, which beef made mine:
all eight presets, not six; multi-select pools on gameplay/prompt/recall because the machine
already draws per knowable; streak and limit as typeable steppers, not fixed chips (beef);
the =preview= axis row (shadow is emitter API); the assistant benched behind an =@beef=
block; no "N overlap dropped" (the resolver dedups); the player is OURS (Rep, chips, judge),
only the handle, the 2 px progress strip, the idle and done cards are the mock's. Responsive
by the SHELL's width, never the window: narrow < 700 one space + stubs, medium < 1040 build +
a side column with set|gameplay tabs, wide all four; height < 520 the drawer is 100 %.
Every wall drags and persists.
