cause: =mod.js= explicitly exports =daemon= (the namespace), and an explicit export
SHADOWS a star export — so lighthouse's =import { daemon }= (the entity schema) silently

ERROR is not absence of a COLLISION — the loser is silently shadowed, not reported.
