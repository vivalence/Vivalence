* method notes

- The authoring session's "every API call precedent-checked" survived at 8 of 9 — the miss
  was not a fabricated API but a WORSE-CHANNEL choice (JSON-path where a column exists).
  Precedent-checking proves a call CAN work, not that it is the system's own answer.
- A pass that "improves the quest" writes into the TANGLE, not into a findings appendix
  the lander must merge by hand — beef's /"compact it back into the quest"/ is the rule:
  the quest document IS the artifact; a pass that leaves its findings beside the code
  instead of in it has not finished. Patch demoted to provenance, tangle canonical, status
  header says so.
- The guard ate =--check= too. Right outcome, wrong reflex: filing ≠ landing, and nothing
  in "work them into our quest system" needed the working tree at all. The dry-run urge
  came from wanting to VERIFY a status line — verification by hunk-read (Read the patch,
  Read the targets, compare) did the same job inside the read-only envelope.
