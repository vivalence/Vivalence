* form, not lemma — and it is reproducible

Comparing stored zipf against =zipf_frequency(lemma, "it")= gave 48% exact / mean drift −0.379. Comparing against =zipf_frequency(surface_form, "it")=: **4998/4998 exact**. The corpus RANKED is form-frequency, minted from this exact package, fully reproducible. beef pinned it:

#+begin_quote
its correct that the frequency rank is from the form not the lemma
#+end_quote

Also verified later, when new words needed authentic ranks: =rank= is the form's position in =top_n_list("it", N)= — pane → 1963 reproduces the stored value. Minting method recovered, not guessed.

A first tooling lesson en route: importing =words/*.js= naively double-counts because =index.js= is a barrel re-exporting everything — 10040 "words" were 2×5020.

* the prune that broke the paradigms behind it

The top-200-by-rank table beef asked for (=gimme a table of lemma form translation by rank of the first 200=) exposed 23 entries at rank 0 — wordfreq-unknown-to-the-list forms like /crederemmo/, /dormivate/, /pattinano/. Under =rank ASC= they sort **first**.

#+begin_quote
ok. problem: a rank 0 fucks with all my algorithms.
i sort by frequency. so now i am learning a bunch of never ever applicative verbs.
prune that.
#+end_quote

I deleted the 23 — then the integrity check found **all 23 referenced by conjugation.js paradigm slots**. A pure delete breaks the Paradigm game's bundles. The corpus already carried the answer: 22 forms (/succedo/, /pioviamo/, /di'/) exist with **no RANKED trait at all** — paradigm-complete, invisible to frequency sort. The 23 were the same class wearing a buggy =rank: 0=.

Recovery had its own lesson: VCS is read-only (the hook blocked even =git show=), Time Machine's snapshot predated the corpus — but =testament/variant/mountpoint/daemon_italian/*.viva.db= held all 6386 entities. The entries were rebuilt from the DB rows (trait JSON authoritative, symbols cloned from paradigm siblings with person/number swapped), RANKED stripped. End state: 45 no-RANKED forms under one convention, zero rank-0, 351 bundles resolving.

Three-corpus comparison: spanish = coherent classroom-A1 (413 lemmas). **brazilian = 629 lemmas of auto-tag drift** — /unicórnio/, /astronauta/, /prostituta/, /subestimar/ marked "survival". Do not copy brazilian survival; it is not curation.

The pin: survival = day-one transactional production, not classroom units, not raw frequency. Then two edit rounds bracketed by QA agents (integrity + pedagogy). The pedagogy agent's best catch was the **two-speed verb regime**: 15 core verbs carried survival on all ~34 forms — subjunctive imperfect of /essere/ as "day one" — while newer verbs used an 8-form template. Trimmed the core 15 to the template (+ /vorrei/, /potrei/), −385 entries. Also out: postal cluster, toiletries, produce singles, six of eight colors, classroom descriptors, frequency adverbs. In: 22 transactional verbs, the complete articulated-preposition grid (30/30), teens numerals, closed-family stragglers (nostro/vostro, questi/queste/quelli/quelle, piena/vuota).

One self-inflicted wound in this stretch: a regex whose keep-predicate doubled as a want-predicate tagged **every** present-indicative bundle in the file (249 of them). Caught by the count check, reverted same turn. The bundle law that survived: survival verb ⟺ its present.indicative bundle tagged, nothing else.

Sentence rank has no empirical referent, so it is **free** — a declared didactic order, not a measurement.
