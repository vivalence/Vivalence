* two findings (durable → m7)
1. *fold's shared-ref guard bites Mode.* =mode.traits= IS =mode.manifest.traits= — the SAME array reference (Mode does =this.traits = this.manifest.traits=). fold's =seen= WeakSet cuts the second occurrence, so top-level =traits= DROPS from the pojo while =manifest.traits= survives. The snapshot is CORRECT; the equality assert was wrong. Locked the behavior: =expect(pojo.traits).toBeUndefined()=.
2. *wire↔instance apertures DRIFT.* Across the 10 paired modes, 2 diverge — =game-nyan= and =homepage-aprende= carry a wire-only =/tool= branch. That is TOOLED mounting =/tool= at INTEGRATION, after the in-process instance fold was captured (=homepage= even shows instance-leaves > wire-leaves). A golden =instance === wire= assert would be FALSELY RED. So #5 was built the SOUND way: assert both sides are well-formed strip contracts (the stripwire claim at the type level), console-REPORT the per-mode drift, never assert byte-equality.
