** what "schematic" turned out to mean, and where it ran out

Read as the typology descriptor — `subsystems/typology/schematics/` — which was right, and
which is where it broke. Only **Buffer has one**: the generic `BufferDescriptor`, plus the
per-mode narrowing every App declares as `v.buffer({ data: … })`. There is no
`v.retention()` and no `v.trace()`; those two entities exist only as MikroORM
`EntitySchema`. His turn-3 ruling settled the asymmetry without needing the finding
explained: /"for buffer, replace schematic with entity."/ The entity is the surface that
cannot lie; the descriptor is documentation checked by nothing outside `v.buffer()`.

** the shape, once it stopped moving

Six entities, then seven, each written *entity → (traits) → snapshot → code*. His note
inside the draft — /"turn all these entity/code/snapshot markers into headers. not just
here."/ — took the bolded markers to `######`, so the outline now carries the structure
rather than the paragraphs doing it by convention. Userspace: Retention · Trace · Buffer ·
Thread. Dataspace: Literal · Symbol · Mode. For the last three the entity slot is
`f(runtime, domain)` — one table, two files, and the domain half named honestly, including
where it is empty.
