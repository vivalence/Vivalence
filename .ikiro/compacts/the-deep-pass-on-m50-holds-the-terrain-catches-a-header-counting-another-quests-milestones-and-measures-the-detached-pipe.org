described — the five =Wafer= subclasses, the two ALIVE producers, the kajuit dossier shape, the =v=

showed 1761 against the parent's 1756.

** a header that lies by arithmetic (row 2)

=quest-report.py= derives =#+progress= by splitting the status line on =.;·|= and marking done every
=M<n>= inside a clause that contains a done-word. m50's status says /"Supersedes m49 M2/M3 (m49 sunset
to done/ with M1 landed)"/ and /"Nothing applied: M1 (…)"/ — =done=, =landed=, =applied= each fire, and
M1 M2 M3 of ANOTHER quest are booked as this quest's. Three of five done, phase wip, next blast — for a
quest with zero milestones landed. The instrument is right about its own rule; the sentence poisoned it.
The fix is a reword (proposed below, beef's header), not a smarter regex: a status line names only its
own milestones inside done-worded clauses.

** the sketch encodes a Status it has not read (row 2)

(=status.js:15,26=) uppercases every code, so a live =die.status.reflection.code= is =ALIVE= and the enum

inline loop in kajuit's =Dataspace= (=new Vector().use(attach…)=, the =use[]= loop, =affect(noop)=) and
