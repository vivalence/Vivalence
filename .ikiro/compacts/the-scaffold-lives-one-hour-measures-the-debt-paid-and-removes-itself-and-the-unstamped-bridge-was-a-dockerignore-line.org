* the instrument defect — the dump debugged its own producer

beef's localhost dump: 8 blocks, 6 of them =1003 · 1003 · 2003 · 1001 · 2081ms= —
quantized to ~1000ms with zero wire activity between. An idle page does not block itself
at exactly 1.003s repeatedly: Firefox throttles rAF to 1Hz when the window is
unfocused/occluded, and the gap sampler read the throttle as blocks. Fixed before
deploy: skip while =document.hidden || !document.hasFocus()=, reset =previous= on
visibility/blur/focus flips. Known side effect, accepted: console-triggered busy loops
don't record in Firefox (focus sits in devtools); page interactions record fine. The
m30 quest carries this as the instrument postscript — all three producer legs
live-verified (loaf/chrome with attribution, rAF-gap/his firefox, longtask emission).
