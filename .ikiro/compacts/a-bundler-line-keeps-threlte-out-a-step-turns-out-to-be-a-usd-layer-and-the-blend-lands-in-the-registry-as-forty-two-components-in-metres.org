* what the assembly package is — and the answer to turn 46

Turn 46 asked whether =fold · stack · resolve · bom= are ORM methods. **They are not.** Both halves exist on purpose:

- =domain/assembly/resolve.js= — PURE, over plain rows. No =em=, no =flush=, chess's =rules/= seat. Takes a =Map= or an object of ={slug, trait}=. The App imports this same file and folds the dataspace's rows in the browser, where there is no ORM at all.
- =LiteralRepository.closure(row)= — an ORM method, and the only one of the four that touches the database: breadth-first over =references(row)= under the row's owner, one query per depth, returning the =Map= the pure functions take. =repository.resolve= and =repository.bom= are then two lines each: =pure.resolve(await this.closure(row), row.slug, opts)=. The test pins the equality: =expect(tree).toEqual(pure(rows, "frame"))=.

The rest, as it stands on disk:

Rulings 6–13, in the quest:

6. *A step is a layer; an assembly is a layer stack.* USD's LayerStack. =resolve(slug, {upto})= is the state after N steps; no intermediate product is a row. =TRANSITIONED= gone.
7. *Operations are entity and repository methods; tools use them.* The =v= shapes type the payloads — one description for =trait{}=, the verb's parameter, the tool's input and the harness. Measured: =v= has no =tuple= and no =lazy=, so nodes are flat.
8. *Three topologies, one per kind.* Ruling 6 moved the seam: assembly and step are the pair, component the odd one.
9. *=@droneaid/topography/model=, version 0.0.0* — /"droneaid model.0000"/.
10. *Rows are metres, Y-up.* =SOURCED.scale= keeps Blender's factor; embed bakes scale, never rotation — one =arm=, four placements.
11. *No OVERRIDDEN trait.* The same path in a later layer IS the over; =active: false= removes. USD's own words.
12. *=COUNTED{qty}= is the kit*; =bom()= is the build. The check is the two disagreeing.
13. *The domain declares what it hardcodes; the owner is the caller's.* =kinds.js= is the declaration; =mint= refuses a trait the kind does not declare, checks every payload against its shape, and names the topology when the root symbol is missing. Chess's =owner(kind)= is GONE.
