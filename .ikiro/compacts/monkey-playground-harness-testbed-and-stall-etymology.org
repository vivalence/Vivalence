Finn: "Go into etymology on the names Stall and Paddock. Consider that one of

- *Stall* — Proto-Germanic =*stallaz= "standing-place" → German =der Stall= =
  stable/barn (the enclosure metaphor is NATIVE, not borrowed). Emacs: =Stall=man
  (RMS, Emacs/GNU founder) — and this codebase does emacs-style buffer switching,
  the Stall IS the buffer cursor. One word, both layers: German stable + Emacs
  patriarch.
- *Paddock* — OE =pearroc= → park/parc; the field BESIDE the stable. Pure
  English, no German, no Emacs.
- Verdict (I flipped my own prior call): KEEP Stall. Renaming Stall→Paddock walks
  the meaning one enclosure outward and drops both connotations. Paddock is
  adjacent; Stall is the thing.
⇒ M8 rename now effectively PARKED/dead — the etymology is the argument against it.
