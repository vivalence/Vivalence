* the shape correction — blast-bracket, not micro-rungs

Beef, verbatim: *"Consider this not as a atomic step by step microprocess, but
as a blast change where we define an outside radius, where we run the tests

adjust the tests, check that the tests still run-in... on integration level

This is the STANDING =blast-bracket= ritual already codified in the rebuilt

① BLAST   the outside radius — every consumer of owner/package, scope.registry
